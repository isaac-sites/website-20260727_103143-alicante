## Introduction

Es Vedrà, the dramatic limestone islet off Ibiza’s western coast, became one of the most recognisable UFO locations in the [Balearic Islands]({{ 'balearic-islands/' | relative_url }}), but its connection with the famous Manises incident is more complicated than many retellings suggest. The key point is that Es Vedrà helped create the setting and mythology around the story, while the documented 11 November 1979 Manises case was primarily an aviation incident investigated through pilot reports, air-traffic information and Spanish military records. There is no strong evidence that the islet itself caused, generated or explained the encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-manises-1979-1979-11-11-17-28-avistamiento-en-valencia-motril-madrid-ii" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente Manises, Avistamientos Valencia–Motril–Madrid II (November 1979), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1979</span></span></span>

The attraction of Es Vedrà as a UFO landmark comes from the combination of a striking landscape, older legends, later paranormal claims and its location near the flight path involved in the Manises episode. Over time, these elements merged into a single popular narrative: a mysterious rock associated with strange lights, unexplained phenomena and an aircraft encounter. Separating the folklore from the documented event is essential for understanding Es Vedrà’s real place in Balearic UFO history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeibiza.com/news/mysteries-es-vedra/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeibiza.com">[Diario de Ibiza]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de IbizaUnveiling the mysteries of Es Vedrà: 7 paranormal legends of Ibiza’s magic islet – Ibiza Daily NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

## How Es Vedrà became a paranormal landmark

Es Vedrà’s reputation did not begin with UFO stories. The uninhabited rock had long been surrounded by traditional legends connected to Ibiza’s cultural landscape. Stories associated it with sea myths, spiritual experiences and unusual natural qualities, helping establish it as a place of mystery before modern UFO accounts appeared. The islet has been linked in popular culture with ideas ranging from ancient mythology to claims of unusual “energy” or magnetic properties, although scientific evidence for paranormal effects remains lacking.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Es_Vedr%C3%A0" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Es Vedrà</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Es Vedrà</span></span></span>

Modern paranormal interest grew around several recurring themes:

* **The “Triangle of Silence” idea:** Some enthusiasts describe a zone connecting Es Vedrà, locations on the Spanish mainland and Mallorca as an area of strange events or navigational problems. These claims are part of local UFO folklore rather than a verified geographical phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.seaexperienceibiza.com/en/travel-guide-ibiza-formentera/41-es-vedra-ibiza-legend%3Fsrsltid%3DAfmBOooFLQAysyO62UMpYu0vCG2r4ZF1Rqb2KM0Gz5cjq-z3rw0I6VXB" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: seaexperienceibiza.com">[seaexperienceibiza.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">seaexperienceibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">5 myths and legends about the islet of Es Vedrà</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">5 myths and legends about the islet of Es Vedrà</span></span></span>
* **Unusual lights and sightings:** Visitors and residents have reported seeing unexplained lights around the islet, contributing to its reputation as an Ibiza UFO hotspot. Such reports are difficult to evaluate because many lack independent witnesses, instrument records or detailed contemporary documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeibiza.com/news/mysteries-es-vedra/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeibiza.com">[Diario de Ibiza]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de IbizaUnveiling the mysteries of Es Vedrà: 7 paranormal legends of Ibiza’s magic islet – Ibiza Daily NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>
* **A dramatic natural setting:** Es Vedrà’s isolated shape, rising sharply from the Mediterranean, makes it an especially powerful visual symbol. A location already associated with mystery can easily become a focus for later stories, especially when a nearby famous event gives those stories a stronger narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ibiza-spotlight.it/guida/es-vedra" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ibiza-spotlight.it">[Ibiza Spotlight]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ibiza-spotlight.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ibiza Spotlight L&#x27;isola rocciosa di Es Vedrà &#124; Ibiza Spotlight</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ibiza Spotlight L&#x27;isola rocciosa di Es Vedrà &#124; Ibiza Spotlight</span></span></span>

The most important distinction is that Es Vedrà’s fame as a paranormal location is largely cultural. Its role in UFO history comes less from a documented series of investigated encounters at the rock itself and more from how later writers and enthusiasts connected it with the nearby Manises case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeibiza.com/news/mysteries-es-vedra/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeibiza.com">[Diario de Ibiza]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de IbizaUnveiling the mysteries of Es Vedrà: 7 paranormal legends of Ibiza’s magic islet – Ibiza Daily NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

## What the Manises flight actually reported

The Manises incident took place on 11 November 1979 and became one of Spain’s best-known UFO cases because it involved a commercial aircraft, a diversion to Valencia’s Manises Airport and a subsequent military response. A TAE Super Caravelle operating a passenger flight reported unusual lights after departing Palma de Mallorca. The crew believed an unidentified object was following or approaching the aircraft, and the captain decided to divert and land at Manises as a precaution. Contemporary reporting recorded the emergency landing and the official investigation that followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elpais.com/diario/1979/11/14/espana/311382029_850215.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elpais.com">[El País]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El PaísEncuesta oficial sobre el aterrizaje forzoso de Manises &#124; España &#124; EL PAÍSNovember 14, 1979...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 14, 1979</span></span></span>

The case attracted particular attention because it included several features unusual for UFO reports:

* communication with aviation authorities;
* an official inquiry;
* a military interception attempt involving a Spanish Air Force fighter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-manises-1979-1979-11-11-17-28-avistamiento-en-valencia-motril-madrid-ii" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente Manises, Avistamientos Valencia–Motril–Madrid II (November 1979), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1979</span></span></span>

The declassified Spanish Air Force documentation shows that the event was investigated as an unidentified phenomenon. The existence of an official file demonstrates that authorities took the report seriously, but it does not demonstrate that the object was extraterrestrial or that Es Vedrà had any physical connection with the event. The category “unidentified” refers to an unresolved observation, not a confirmed explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-manises-1979-1979-11-11-17-28-avistamiento-en-valencia-motril-madrid-ii" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente Manises, Avistamientos Valencia–Motril–Madrid II (November 1979), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1979</span></span></span>

A major reason the Es Vedrà connection became popular is geography. Some accounts place the first unusual observations near Ibiza and then connect the flight’s route with the islet, turning a nearby location into the centre of the mystery. However, the available documentation concerns the aircraft encounter and subsequent investigation rather than evidence of activity originating from Es Vedrà itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-manises-1979-1979-11-11-17-28-avistamiento-en-valencia-motril-madrid-ii" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente Manises, Avistamientos Valencia–Motril–Madrid II (November 1979), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa ·...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 1979</span></span></span>

## Where later retellings outran the documentary record

The Manises story grew considerably after 1979. Popular books, magazine articles and tourism-focused accounts often presented Es Vedrà as a kind of UFO gateway or source of unexplained phenomena. These versions created a stronger link between the rock and the aircraft incident than the surviving records support.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeibiza.com/news/mysteries-es-vedra/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeibiza.com">[Diario de Ibiza]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de IbizaUnveiling the mysteries of Es Vedrà: 7 paranormal legends of Ibiza’s magic islet – Ibiza Daily NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

**“The UFO came from Es Vedrà.”**

This is a popular interpretation, not an established finding. ## Es Vedrà’s real place in Balearic UFO history

Es Vedrà matters because it shows how UFO legends are built. A visually extraordinary place, older folklore traditions and a genuine aviation mystery combined to create one of the Balearic Islands’ most memorable paranormal narratives. The rock became a symbol of mystery, while the Manises incident provided the dramatic event that gave the legend a wider audience.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeibiza.com/news/mysteries-es-vedra/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeibiza.com">[Diario de Ibiza]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeibiza.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de IbizaUnveiling the mysteries of Es Vedrà: 7 paranormal legends of Ibiza’s magic islet – Ibiza Daily NewsJuly 2, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2, 2025</span></span></span>

From an evidence perspective, the two should be separated. The Manises case remains an important Spanish UFO investigation because it involved aviation professionals and official scrutiny. Es Vedrà remains important because of its cultural role as a UFO landmark and a focus of local storytelling.
