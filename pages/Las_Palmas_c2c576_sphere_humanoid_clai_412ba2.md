## Introduction

The claim that two witnesses near Gáldar saw a transparent sphere containing tall humanoid figures is the most memorable part of the 22 June 1976 Canary Islands incident, but it is not the best-supported part. The detailed “occupant” account came chiefly from a doctor travelling to an urgent house call and the taxi driver beside him. A third passenger saw unusual light but did not see the supposed sphere’s interior, while the many other witnesses across Gran Canaria and neighbouring islands mainly described a distant luminous halo, coloured cloud or expanding circle in the sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

That distinction matters. The wider light display was independently observed from several locations and by Spanish naval personnel. The nearby craft, platform and humanoids were not. Even the Air Force investigator who considered the doctor and driver sincere concluded that their extraordinary description lacked sufficient confirmation and should not be accepted as established fact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

## What the doctor and taxi driver reported

Doctor Francisco Julio Padrón was travelling by taxi towards Las Rosas, in the Gáldar area, after being called to attend an elderly patient. According to his statement in the Spanish Air Force file, the vehicle was moving slowly up a rough local road when its headlights illuminated what he took to be a dim, stationary sphere close to the ground. He estimated that it was only 15 to 30 metres away.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

Padrón’s description was unusually elaborate. He said the sphere was transparent enough for stars to remain visible through it and had a faint electric-blue appearance. In its lower portion, he reported seeing an aluminium-coloured platform, three large consoles and two figures facing one another. He estimated the figures at roughly 2.5 to 3 metres tall, with long torsos, comparatively short legs, elongated heads or helmets, pointed-looking hands and rigid, bright red clothing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

He further claimed that bluish vapour emerged from a central tube and spread around the sphere’s interior. The sphere then appeared to expand to the apparent size of a twenty-storey building, although the platform and figures remained unchanged in size. Padrón said it rose slowly, moved towards Tenerife and finally accelerated while changing shape and leaving luminous blue and white effects behind it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

The taxi driver’s formal account supported the basic outline but contained much less anatomical and mechanical detail. He described a transparent object perhaps 25 to 30 metres wide, with two tall, thin people in bright red clothing inside. Because he was driving, he said he had only been able to make out the two figures. He compared them to very tall northern European tourists and said they looked as though they might be seated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

## Why the occupant story lacked independent support

The [official file]({{ 'official-file-fb8c33/' | relative_url }}) separates the doctor-and-driver episode from the much broader regional sighting. That separation is crucial. Numerous witnesses reported an expanding circle, halo, bright patch, blue bands or a changing light. Almost none independently supplied the doctor’s combination of a nearby craft, metallic platform, consoles and sharply described occupants.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

A third person was travelling in the same taxi, but the vehicle roof obstructed his view. He reported seeing only exterior light and noticing the doctor’s and driver’s nervousness. He therefore did not independently confirm either the transparent structure or the humanoid figures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

A labourer living about 200 metres from the supposed close encounter described a fire-like patch, two blue stripes and a ring resembling a lunar halo. That testimony is useful because it supports the presence of a conspicuous luminous phenomenon in the same area, but it does not support a landed or hovering sphere containing occupants.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

One nearby woman did report a large transparent blue ball and said she thought she saw two man-like forms inside. Her statement, however, was explicitly uncertain: she could not be sure, became frightened, shut her doors and windows and stopped watching. In the Air Force summary, this was presented as a hesitant impression rather than a detailed independent observation of humanoids.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

Other local witnesses described the same broad visual family without occupants. A couple at Boca Barranco saw a headlamp-like light that grew to several times the apparent diameter of the Moon and left a blue arc. A teacher in Agaete saw a large transparent circumference through which stars were visible, crossed by two luminous rays. A taxi driver elsewhere near Gáldar saw a circular illuminated ring with fire below it. These accounts strengthen the case that something visually impressive was in the sky, but they make the doctor’s close-range interpretation less rather than more representative of the witness population.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

There was also no physical evidence securely tied to the alleged sphere. The file’s map referred to a supposed landing area in an onion field, but recorded that the claim was checked and found false. No verified ground marks, material residue, photograph of the occupants or instrument reading established that a solid object had been positioned beside the road.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

## The Air Force did not endorse the humanoids

The declassified file is sometimes cited as though the Spanish military officially confirmed a craft and occupants. It did not. The investigator accepted that a real aerial phenomenon had been widely observed, but treated the doctor-and-driver narrative as a separate and doubtful component.

His report briefly explored what would follow if their account were taken literally, including speculative ideas about an unusual exploration vehicle and electromagnetic propulsion. Those passages reveal the investigator testing the internal implications of the story; they were not his final finding. He then stated that it was “very probable” that the two men, confronted by an unusual sight in the sky, had described what their minds caused them to see while influencing each other.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

The investigator carefully distinguished sincerity from accuracy. He said he had no doubt about the witnesses’ seriousness or honesty: they had recounted what they genuinely believed they saw. Nevertheless, he found reasonable doubt about whether the detailed scene had existed objectively. His formal conclusion was that the doctor’s and taxi driver’s statements could not be given unquestioned credibility and, without conclusive confirmation, had to be rejected for the time being.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

This distinction remains the fairest way to read the case. Calling the witnesses dishonest goes beyond the evidence. Calling their account independently verified also goes beyond the evidence.

## Sincerity, perception and mistaken distance

The official inquiry included a medical discussion of the two men’s state of mind, but that material requires caution. A doctor associated with the [Las Palmas]({{ 'las-palmas/' | relative_url }}) Parapsychology Society said that Padrón appeared to be under intense emotional strain while recounting the incident, yet firmly rejected the idea that he was staging a fraud. The same doctor suggested that an unexpected aerial display, darkness, fatigue and mutual influence might have produced an altered interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

Some of that assessment rested on an examination of the iris, claims about neurosis and proposed hypnosis sessions. These are not strong modern methods for deciding whether a reported visual event was objectively present. The medical annex therefore cannot prove that either witness hallucinated. Its real value is narrower: it shows that the investigators themselves recognised a possible psychological or perceptual gap between an honest report and a reliable reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

Ordinary visual ambiguity offers a less dramatic possibility. At night, an observer looking across open ground or sea may have few dependable cues for distance and scale. A large illuminated cloud far away can appear to sit just beyond a ridge or field. Once a witness estimates that the light is only metres away, irregular bright and dark regions within it may be interpreted as a platform, equipment or bodies. Apparent growth can likewise result from an expanding luminous cloud rather than an approaching solid sphere.

The testimony itself contains signs of uncertain distance. Padrón said the sphere was near the ground but could not tell whether it touched it. He also described the sphere growing enormously while the supposed occupants and platform retained their original apparent size. That behaviour is difficult to reconcile with a single transparent solid craft expanding around fixed passengers. It is easier to understand as two perceptual layers: relatively stable bright features set against a rapidly enlarging illuminated cloud or halo.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

The figures may also have emerged from the structure of the light. Several witnesses independently reported two lines, bands or rays within a transparent circular form. The doctor and driver converted a broadly comparable two-part pattern into tall red people, while another frightened observer only thought she might have seen two human-like shapes. This does not demonstrate exactly what they perceived, but it provides a plausible route from a real luminous stimulus to a populated “craft” interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

## Did later reporting strengthen the claim?

Later retellings generally made the humanoid scene more prominent, not more secure. Newspapers, UFO books and anniversary coverage naturally favoured the vivid image of two giants inside a blue sphere over the less dramatic account of an expanding light. Recent local reporting still identifies Padrón’s testimony as the element that transformed a regional sky display into the celebrated Gáldar encounter, while also acknowledging that other people present did not all report the same details.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://infonortedigital.com/art/192465/50-anos-del-ovni-de-galdar-el-misterio-de-las-rosas-que-sigue-mirando-al-cielo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: infonortedigital.com">[InfonorteDigital]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">infonortedigital.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">50 años del OVNI de Gáldar: el misterio de Las Rosas que sigue mirando al cielo &#124; Información del Noroeste de Gran Canari...</span></span></span>

The release of the full Air Force file in the 1990s strengthened knowledge of what the doctor and taxi driver originally said. It showed that their claims were not invented decades later and that both men gave formal statements. It also weakened exaggerated versions of the story by exposing the internal limits of the evidence: the obstructed third passenger, the absence of physical confirmation, the uncertainty of the nearby woman and the investigator’s refusal to validate the occupant account. Spain’s Ministry of Defence now makes its declassified UFO files available through the Virtual Defence Library.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The file also reveals how easily later summaries can blur separate levels of evidence. The official conclusion that an unidentified aerial phenomenon had occurred referred principally to the widely witnessed light display. It did not certify the doctor’s sphere, its supposed landing position or the existence of its occupants. ## The most defensible assessment

The transparent-sphere story cannot be dismissed simply because it sounds extraordinary. Two people did make broadly similar formal statements, and the doctor’s version remained detailed and consistent when a recorded interview was compared with his testimony to the investigator. Their evident emotion and the professional risk of publicising such an account support personal sincerity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

But sincerity is not independent corroboration. The men observed the event together, communicated during it and estimated distance without reliable measurements. The third passenger did not see the claimed interior. Nearby witnesses confirmed unusual luminosity but not the full craft-and-occupant scene. No physical trace or contemporary image established a sphere close to the ground. The Air Force investigator ultimately rejected the close-encounter claim as unconfirmed even while accepting the broader aerial phenomenon as real.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-es-expediente-canarias-1976-06-22-1976-06-22-avistamiento-en-canarias" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyExpediente, Avistamiento en Canarias (22 June 1976), Ejército del Aire / Ministerio de Defensa · 1976 · UFO Transparency...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 1976</span></span></span>

Within the UFO history of Las Palmas, the humanoid account is therefore best classified as a sincere but weakly supported interpretation attached to a much stronger [mass sighting]({{ 'mass-sighting/' | relative_url }}). It explains why Gáldar became famous, but it should not be treated as the evidential centre of the 1976 incident. The enduring lesson is not that two giants were proved to have visited Gran Canaria. It is that a real and startling light display could be perceived at radically different apparent distances, producing one of Spain’s most vivid—and least independently secured—occupant stories.
