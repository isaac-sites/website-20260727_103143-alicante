## Introduction

Puente Almuhey is the strongest official UFO case near [Palencia]({{ 'why-palencias-ufo-record-remains-so/' | relative_url }}), but it was not, in any straightforward administrative sense, a Palencia case. The Spanish Air Force file identifies the location as Puente Almuhey in León and states that the observations occurred in the immediate area of that village. The confusion arose because the first sighting, on 24 November 1968, took place on a road leading towards Palencia and close to the provincial boundary. Later summaries sometimes allowed that directional detail to become a change of province.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/download/SpanishUFOFiles/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_%28leon%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI)AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI) AVISTAMIENTO D...</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because Palencia has very little comparable case-grade material in Spain’s declassified military archive. Puente Almuhey is therefore tempting to adopt as the province’s nearest landmark incident. The fairest description is more precise: it was a León investigation with a genuine Palencia-border connection, useful for understanding Palencia’s regional UFO history but not evidence of a documented sighting inside Palencia itself.

## The 1968 sightings and official inquiry

The file covers three dates: 24 November, 8 December and 10 December 1968. Although the reports differed in appearance and duration, officials grouped them together because they occurred near the same village, at broadly similar evening hours and involved unexplained luminous phenomena. The surviving summary describes two main types of observation: a reddish light close to road level on 24 November, and white or yellowish lights seen near or above Puente Almuhey on 8 and 10 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://planetabenitez.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_leon.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: planetabenitez.com">[J.J. Benítez]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">planetabenitez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 11 24 avistamiento en almuhey leon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BenítezAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNIAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI) AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENO...</span></span></span>

The first episode was the most dramatic. At about 10pm on 24 November, three people reportedly saw a reddish luminous object on or beside the road. Two of the witnesses were a married couple; the third was a young mechanic who was not connected with them. The published file retains two of their statements. One account described a disc roughly two metres across, almost at ground level, moving very slowly and apparently dividing into three parts before forming again. The other described a luminous shape that altered into something resembling a horn or triangle before fading.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

Those accounts agree on several broad features—red colour, low apparent position, slow or absent movement and a duration of roughly ten to fifteen minutes—but disagree on the object’s changing shape. That discrepancy is important. It does not prove that nothing unusual was seen, but it shows how uncertain estimates of shape and structure can become when witnesses observe a bright light at night without a reliable measure of distance. The official summary itself highlighted the difference between the “disc” and “horn” descriptions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/stream/SpanishUFOFiles/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_%28leon%29_djvu.txt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveFull text of &quot;Spanish UFO Files&quot;Full text of &quot;Spanish UFO Files&quot; Full text of &quot; Spanish UFO Files &quot; See other formats AVI...</span></span></span>

The second cluster occurred on 8 December, generally placed between 7.30pm and 8pm. Three witnesses, including the stationmaster and another railway employee, reported a bright white or yellowish light near the village. Descriptions ranged from circular or oval to lantern-like. Some witnesses estimated an apparent height of 400 to 500 metres and said the phenomenon remained visible for up to two hours, although those figures were subjective rather than instrumentally measured.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

A similar light was reported again on 10 December. The two railway workers said that the phenomenon returned, while another local witness reported a much shorter observation of about two minutes. This created one of the file’s clearest conflicts: witnesses apparently looking at the same general phenomenon gave durations ranging from minutes to approximately an hour and a half.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

The official inquiry began because the stationmaster wrote to the León military aerodrome. His letter was received on 13 December, and Air Force officers travelled to Puente Almuhey on 14 December to interview witnesses. This gives the case more value than a story reconstructed only decades later: statements were taken within days of the final observation, and the investigation produced a formal summary, a witness table and an assessment sent through the Air Force chain of command.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

It was nevertheless a limited investigation. The later declassification summary says that the original work essentially consisted of interviewing witnesses. There was no radar confirmation, photograph, physical trace, recovered material or calibrated measurement of direction, height or distance. The Air Force meteorologist found no comparable atmospheric phenomenon reported for the relevant dates, but that check ruled out only the meteorological possibilities considered at the time; it did not establish that the lights were artificial craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

## Why the Palencia road caused confusion

The border dispute begins with a simple geographical phrase. Accounts of the 24 November observation commonly say that it occurred on the road from Puente Almuhey towards Palencia. Read casually, that can sound like the witnesses had crossed into Palencia. The official documentation does not say that. Its cover identifies the place as Puente Almuhey, León, while the investigators described the observations as occurring in an area immediately surrounding the village.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/download/SpanishUFOFiles/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_%28leon%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI)AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI) AVISTAMIENTO D...</span></span></span>

Puente Almuhey belongs to the municipality of Valderrueda in León. The municipality’s own administrative address places Puente Almuhey in León, confirming the basic jurisdiction that appears in the military file.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://aytovalderrueda.sedelectronica.es/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aytovalderrueda.sedelectronica.es">[aytovalderrueda.sedelectronica.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aytovalderrueda.sedelectronica.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ayuntamiento de Valderrueda</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">InicioAyuntamiento de Valderrueda - Inicio Ayuntamiento de Valderrueda Horario de Atención al público: 09:00 a 14:00 Calle Juan Ferrero...</span></span></span>

The ambiguity is strengthened by the landscape. Puente Almuhey lies near the Palencia boundary, and later León reporting located the sightings around La Espina, a meeting of hills close to the provincial limit. One newspaper account described the investigated coordinates as coinciding with the road boundary area between León and Palencia. That makes “border case” a defensible label, but it still does not demonstrate that any witness was standing inside Palencia or that the reported light occupied Palencia’s airspace.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodeleon.es/leon/220518/605054/ee-uu-reconoce-primera-vez-expedientes-ovni-espana-tres-leon.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodeleon.es">[Diario de León]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodeleon.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de LeónEE UU reconoce por primera vez expedientes ovni y España lo hace con tres en LeónEE UU reconoce por primera vez expedientes...</span></span></span>

The typed and scanned coordinates in reproductions of the file are difficult to read and contain apparent transcription or typographical irregularities. The official table refers to kilometre 33 on the road between Cervera and La Magdalena and notes the provincial boundary, but it does not supply the kind of modern mapped position that would settle the location to a particular side of the line.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/stream/SpanishUFOFiles/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_%28leon%29_djvu.txt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet ArchiveFull text of &quot;Spanish UFO Files&quot;Full text of &quot;Spanish UFO Files&quot; Full text of &quot; Spanish UFO Files &quot; See other formats AVI...</span></span></span>

## What the surviving file can establish

The most secure conclusion is that several local people reported unusual lights over a period of just over two weeks and that their reports prompted an official Air Force inquiry. The case was not created solely by later UFO writers. A railway stationmaster initiated contact with military authorities, officers interviewed witnesses promptly, and the resulting papers were preserved within the Air Force’s collection of reports on unusual aerial phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

The file also shows a degree of independent witness structure. The first sighting involved a couple and a separate young man; the December sightings involved railway workers and other residents. This is more persuasive than a single unsupported recollection. However, “independent” should not be overstated in a small community after an unusual event had become locally known. By 8 and 10 December, witnesses may have been alert to the possibility of seeing the phenomenon again, and some people observed it together.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

The evidence is strongest for the existence of sincerely reported lights and weakest for claims about their physical form. Estimated diameters of 30 centimetres, 40 centimetres or two metres are not meaningful without a known distance. A distant planet, nearby lamp or intermediate light can appear to have very different sizes depending on what distance the observer assumes. The same limitation applies to estimates that a light was 300 or 500 metres high. No triangulation or simultaneous measurements were made.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://planetabenitez.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/1968-11-24_avistamiento_en_almuhey_leon.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: planetabenitez.com">[J.J. Benítez]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">planetabenitez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 11 24 avistamiento en almuhey leon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BenítezAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNIAVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOS (EXPEDIENTES OVNI) AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENO...</span></span></span>

The Air Force’s conclusion was appropriately cautious. Because of the discrepancies in the testimony, investigators said they could not determine the nature of the objects. They considered, only as remote possibilities, the setting Moon for the first incident and a planet seen through poor visibility or low cloud for the later lights. This wording matters: the file did not positively identify the sightings, but neither did it endorse an exotic explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

The astronomical suggestions have limitations of their own. A waxing crescent Moon was present on 24 November 1968, so the general idea of a low celestial object was not arbitrary. Yet the reported time, reddish colour, alleged proximity to the road and descriptions of changing form would require a careful reconstruction of direction, horizon, cloud and terrain before a lunar explanation could be considered convincing. The original file did not contain that level of astronomical analysis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://theskylive.com/moon/1968" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theskylive.com">[The Sky Live]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theskylive.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on theskylive.com.</span></span></span>

The December observations sound more compatible with a bright astronomical object because witnesses described a long-lasting white or yellow light with slow or no obvious movement. Distortion by haze, low cloud and the “autokinetic effect”—the apparent wandering of a stationary point of light in darkness—can make stars and planets seem mobile or structured. However, the file lacks sufficiently reliable bearings and timings to identify one specific celestial source retrospectively. The planetary explanation therefore remains plausible rather than demonstrated.

## Did later reporting strengthen the case?

Later reporting strengthened public access to the incident but did not add decisive new evidence. The Ministry of Defence’s digitisation programme made the file available as part of a collection of 80 case files covering unusual phenomena investigated between 1962 and 1995. The archive explains that files may contain summaries, interviews, meteorological information and recommendations, while personal information about witnesses and officers is withheld.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

Local news coverage in 2016 accurately drew attention to the three observation dates, the railway connection and the military interviews. A 2022 report also revisited the case, referred to earlier press coverage and presented it as one of León’s declassified UFO files. These reports help preserve the regional history, but they largely retell the military papers rather than provide new radar data, photographs or independent records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

The later articles occasionally differ over the number of witnesses or testimonies. One account refers to eight testimonies but notes that only six individuals appear in the published material because some people observed more than one episode; another describes seven witnesses. These differences are more likely to reflect counting methods, redactions and repeated observations than a major contradiction about the event itself. They do, however, warn against repeating neat witness totals without explaining how they were calculated.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ileon.eldiario.es/actualidad/defensa-desclasifica-expediente-x-avistamientos-ovni-puente-almuhey_1_9458017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ileon.eldiario.es">[ileon.eldiario.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ileon.eldiario.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">defensa desclasifica expediente x avistamientos ovni puente almuhey 1 9458017</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Defensa desclasifica un &#x27;Expediente X&#x27; de avistamientos OVNI en Puente Almuhey en 1968...</span></span></span>

No later material located in the public record turns Puente Almuhey into a confirmed Palencia incident. On the contrary, modern access to the cover sheet and investigation summary makes the León classification clearer. The Palencia connection survives because the first event occurred on the road towards the province and because the landscape straddles a close border zone—not because the Air Force registered the file under Palencia.

## The case’s proper place in Palencia’s UFO history

Puente Almuhey belongs in a Palencia UFO history as a neighbouring official case that illustrates the hazards of geographical retelling. It is probably the nearest substantial declassified investigation and offers a useful contrast with Palencia’s more fragmentary sighting tradition. It should not be presented as proof that Palencia itself hosted one of the Spanish Air Force’s major investigated incidents.

The case is also a good example of what “unresolved” means in historical UFO research. It does not mean that an extraordinary craft was established. It means that officers could not confidently identify reports that were already imprecise and internally inconsistent. The absence of an agreed explanation is real, but so are the missing bearings, uncertain distances, conflicting durations and lack of physical or instrumental evidence.

The most accurate verdict is therefore geographical and evidential at the same time: Puente Almuhey was a genuine official UFO inquiry in León, conducted close enough to Palencia for the province to form part of the story. The border explains the association; it does not transfer the case.
