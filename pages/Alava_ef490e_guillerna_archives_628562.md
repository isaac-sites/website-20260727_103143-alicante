## Introduction

José Luis Guillerna is one of the most important local figures in the history of UFO research in Álava because he helped preserve a large body of regional reports that might otherwise have disappeared. Beginning in the late 1970s, Guillerna collected testimonies from people who said they had observed unusual lights, objects, or events in the province’s skies, building a private archive of interviews, notes, and case files. His work did not establish that these reports had an extraordinary cause, but it created an important record of how UFO stories developed in Álava and how witnesses, researchers, and the media interpreted unexplained observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eitb.eus/es/audios/detalle/2316244/avistamientos-ovnis-alava-jose-luis-guillerna-35-anos-investigando/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eitb.eus">[EITB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eitb.eus</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamientos OVNIS en Alava. ## Guillerna’s role in local UFO research

### A collector of Álava’s unexplained sightings

Guillerna became known as a pioneer of UFO investigation in Álava after spending decades gathering accounts from residents who believed they had witnessed unusual aerial phenomena. His research involved interviewing witnesses and compiling reports during a period when interest in UFOs was particularly strong in Spain. According to a later profile by Euskal Irrati Telebista (EITB), Guillerna had spent around 35 years collecting testimonies of alleged [sightings]({{ 'sightings-86791c/' | relative_url }}) in the province, including reports of strange lights and other unusual observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.eitb.eus/es/audios/detalle/2316244/avistamientos-ovnis-alava-jose-luis-guillerna-35-anos-investigando/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: eitb.eus">[EITB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">eitb.eus</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamientos OVNIS en Alava. José Luis Guillerna 35 años investigando</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamientos OVNIS en Alava. José Luis Guillerna 35 años investigandoJune 10, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 10, 2014</span></span></span>

His approach reflected the methods used by many civilian UFO investigators of the period. Before online databases and widespread digital recording, investigators depended on direct contact with witnesses, handwritten notes, sketches, newspaper archives, and published case reports. This created valuable historical material, but it also meant that many cases depended heavily on memory and personal interpretation rather than on independent measurements or physical evidence.

Guillerna’s importance within Álava’s UFO history comes from this preservation role. A single witness account may be difficult to evaluate decades later, but a collection of many reports can show patterns: where sightings were concentrated, what descriptions were common, and how local communities responded to unusual experiences.

## Testimonies, notes, and newspaper records

Several Álava cases associated with Guillerna became part of wider Spanish UFO discussions through specialist publications and investigator networks. One example is the investigation of the La Hoz case, a reported 1981 incident near the Sierra de Árcamo area. The case was investigated through witness interviews connected with Guillerna’s research activity, although later readers have to assess it through the surviving testimony rather than through confirmed physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/1a-epoca-cuadernos-de-ufologia-no-08-1984/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO Magazine ArchiveCdU — Cuadernos de Ufologia (Fundacion Anomalia) &#124; UFO Magazine ArchiveDecember 1, 1984...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1, 1984</span></span></span>

Another important example is the Alegría de Álava case, which became the subject of discussion among Spanish UFO researchers during the 1980s. Accounts connected with the case were collected by investigators including José Luis Guillerna, José María Gascón, and Luis Alfonso Gámez, and later publications debated how much confidence should be placed in witness testimony and interpretation methods. The discussion itself is significant because it shows that UFO research in Álava was not simply a matter of collecting stories; investigators also argued about standards of evidence and how unusual claims should be analysed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufomagazines.com/1a-epoca-cuadernos-de-ufologia-no-10-11-1985/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufomagazines.com">[UFO Magazine Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufomagazines.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ufomagazines.com.</span></span></span>

## What the archive can show — and what it cannot


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Guillerna’s archive is most useful as a record of reports, not as automatic proof of the explanations sometimes attached to them. UFO investigations often begin with a simple question: what did a person observe? Answering a second question — what caused the observation? — requires additional evidence such as photographs, radar records, astronomical data, aircraft information, or independent witnesses.

</div>

This distinction is important when assessing private UFO archives. A carefully recorded testimony can demonstrate that a person genuinely reported seeing something unusual. It does not necessarily demonstrate that the object was a spacecraft, unknown technology, or a phenomenon beyond conventional explanation.

The same issue appears in official UFO records. Spain’s released Air Force UFO files show that authorities investigated reports of unidentified aerial observations, but an unidentified report means that investigators could not establish an explanation from the available information at that time. It does not by itself confirm an extraordinary origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/espanol/ovnis" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives¿Registros muestran prueba de ovnis? &#124; National Archives</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">February 9, 2018...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 9, 2018</span></span></span>

For Guillerna’s collection, the strongest historical contribution is therefore documentary rather than evidential in the strict scientific sense. His work preserved voices, locations, dates, and narratives that help researchers understand the development of UFO culture in Álava.

## The historical value of a private UFO archive

Private archives like Guillerna’s occupy an unusual position between folklore, journalism, and historical documentation. They preserve experiences that official institutions often did not record in detail, especially before the digital era. For local history, that makes them valuable: they reveal what communities considered mysterious, how stories spread, and how investigators attempted to organise uncertainty.

At the same time, private archives require careful reading. Many UFO reports collected in the twentieth century were never tested with modern investigative tools, and some may have ordinary explanations such as aircraft, astronomical objects, atmospheric effects, or mistaken perceptions. The absence of a confirmed explanation does not necessarily indicate that the most unusual explanation is correct.

Guillerna’s legacy in Álava’s UFO history is therefore best understood as that of an archivist of experiences.     if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
