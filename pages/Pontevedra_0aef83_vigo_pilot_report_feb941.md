## Introduction

The Vigo offshore UFO report is one of [Pontevedra]({{ 'pontevedras-ufo-cases-between-mystery/' | relative_url }})’s most notable aviation-related cases because the main witnesses were not people observing from the ground, but airline crew members who said they saw an unusual object over the Atlantic on 12 March 1997. The incident involved an Air Europa flight travelling from New York to Madrid, with the reported object seen west of Vigo while the aircraft was cruising at high altitude. ## The 1997 Atlantic sighting

According to accounts published in 2019, pilot Juan Reyes was commanding Air Europa flight 118 on a transatlantic route when the crew noticed an unusual object over the Atlantic near the Vigo area. The reported position was around 90 kilometres west of Vigo, with the aircraft flying at approximately 11,800 metres altitude. Reyes described seeing a large circular structure with strong lights, comparing its apparent size to the island of Gran Canaria when viewed from above.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlantico.net/vigo/ovni-frente-vigo-tamano-isla-canaria_1_20190416-1659787.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlantico.net">[Atlántico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlantico.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovni frente vigo tamano isla canaria 1 20190416 1659787</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AtlánticoDos pilotos de avión vieron un OVNI frente a la costa de Vigo del tamaño de una isla canaria en 1997April 17, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2019</span></span></span>

The report became unusual because, according to the pilot’s account, the observation was not limited to one person. The crew contacted air traffic control, and Madrid control reportedly asked another aircraft following the same route to check the area. Later retellings state that the second aircraft’s crew also reported seeing something unusual. This alleged cross-check is one of the main reasons the case continues to attract attention among UFO researchers and aviation enthusiasts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlantico.net/vigo/ovni-frente-vigo-tamano-isla-canaria_1_20190416-1659787.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlantico.net">[Atlántico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlantico.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovni frente vigo tamano isla canaria 1 20190416 1659787</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AtlánticoDos pilotos de avión vieron un OVNI frente a la costa de Vigo del tamaño de una isla canaria en 1997April 17, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2019</span></span></span>

However, the public evidence is largely based on later interviews and media reports rather than a complete released investigation file. The existence of a pilot testimony is significant, but the precise details needed for a modern technical assessment — such as full flight data, radar information, atmospheric conditions and independent sensor records — are not publicly available.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlantico.net/vigo/ovni-frente-vigo-tamano-isla-canaria_1_20190416-1659787.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlantico.net">[Atlántico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlantico.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovni frente vigo tamano isla canaria 1 20190416 1659787</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AtlánticoDos pilotos de avión vieron un OVNI frente a la costa de Vigo del tamaño de una isla canaria en 1997April 17, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why aviation witnesses attract attention

Pilot reports occupy a special place in UFO discussions because professional aviators are trained to recognise aircraft, weather effects, navigation hazards and unusual situations. A pilot is generally more experienced than an ordinary observer at judging altitude, distance and movement. This is why aviation cases often receive more attention than brief reports from unidentified witnesses on the ground.

At the same time, aviation experience does not remove all uncertainty. Pilots can still encounter situations where distance, darkness, reflections, cloud layers or limited reference points make an object difficult to identify. A distant light over the ocean is particularly challenging because there may be few visual markers to judge scale or position. In a case such as Vigo, the central question is not whether the witnesses were credible people, but whether the information available allows their interpretation of what they saw to be tested.

The Vigo report therefore illustrates an important distinction in UFO investigations: a reliable witness can provide reliable testimony that something unusual was observed, while the identity of that thing may remain unknown. The strongest part of the case is the quality of the observers and the claimed involvement of more than one aircraft crew; the weakest part is the lack of publicly available physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.atlantico.net/vigo/ovni-frente-vigo-tamano-isla-canaria_1_20190416-1659787.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: atlantico.net">[Atlántico]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">atlantico.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovni frente vigo tamano isla canaria 1 20190416 1659787</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AtlánticoDos pilotos de avión vieron un OVNI frente a la costa de Vigo del tamaño de una isla canaria en 1997April 17, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 17, 2019</span></span></span>

## Limits of the available information

The main difficulty in evaluating the Vigo sighting is the gap between the dramatic description and the limited technical record available to the public.     var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
