## Introduction

Mallorca’s late-1970s UFO craze was less a single unexplained event than a social phenomenon built around one powerful setting: the Serra de Tramuntana and, above all, the summit of Puig Major. In 1978, reports of strange lights, rumours about the military installation on the mountain, newspaper coverage and organised night-time observations combined to turn scattered sightings into a public spectacle. The most famous moment was the Puig Major sky watch of June 1978, when hundreds of enthusiasts gathered around the Cúber reservoir area hoping to witness a supposed extraterrestrial contact. No confirmed object appeared, but the gathering became one of the defining episodes in Mallorca’s UFO history.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2023/02/19/1884911/ovnis-mallorca-hace-mas-anos-desato-ovnimania-avistamientos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.es">[Ultima Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ultima HoraOvnis en Mallorca &#124; Hace más de 45 años se desató una ‘ovnimanía&#x27; de avistamientosFebruary 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2023</span></span></span>

## The 1978 Puig Major gathering

The best-known episode of Mallorca’s UFO fever took place on the night of 22 June 1978, when UFO enthusiasts organised a large observation event near Puig Major and the Cúber area. Contemporary and later local accounts describe a crowd travelling into the mountains to watch the sky, expecting some form of contact or unusual appearance. Estimates in popular retellings vary considerably, with some claims reaching several thousand people, while more cautious accounts describe a gathering of several hundred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2023/02/19/1884911/ovnis-mallorca-hace-mas-anos-desato-ovnimania-avistamientos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.es">[Ultima Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ultima HoraOvnis en Mallorca &#124; Hace más de 45 años se desató una ‘ovnimanía&#x27; de avistamientosFebruary 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2023</span></span></span>

The event was not an official scientific observation programme. It was a public sky watch driven by the expectations of UFO researchers and enthusiasts, influenced by the wider late-1970s fascination with extraterrestrial themes. The success of films such as *Close Encounters of the Third Kind* helped create a cultural atmosphere in which the idea of a dramatic encounter seemed more plausible to many members of the public. In Mallorca, this enthusiasm merged with local stories about unusual lights over the mountains and the belief that Puig Major was a special location.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabalears.cat/societat/ovnis-extraterrestres-balears-ufologia-mallorca-puig-major-vedra-tramuntana_1_1181773.amp.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabalears.cat">[arabalears.cat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabalears.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Les Illes, terra d’ovnis?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Les Illes, terra d’ovnis?</span></span></span>

The night itself ended without the dramatic event many participants hoped for. No spacecraft, communication attempt or unexplained display was recorded. Yet the failure did not end interest. Instead, the gathering became part of the mythology of Mallorca’s UFO movement: a moment when belief, curiosity and local identity came together around a shared search of the night sky.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2023/02/19/1884911/ovnis-mallorca-hace-mas-anos-desato-ovnimania-avistamientos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.es">[Ultima Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ultima HoraOvnis en Mallorca &#124; Hace más de 45 años se desató una ‘ovnimanía&#x27; de avistamientosFebruary 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why military sites and mountain skies fuelled speculation

Puig Major became a natural focus for UFO stories because it combined two ingredients that frequently appear in modern UFO folklore: restricted military facilities and a landscape suited to distant observation. The mountain hosts the Spanish Air Force’s Escuadrón de Vigilancia Aérea Número 7 (EVA 7), an air surveillance unit responsible for monitoring airspace. The existence of radar equipment and restricted areas encouraged speculation among UFO enthusiasts who interpreted secrecy as evidence that something unusual might be occurring.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodemallorca.es/mallorca/2023/11/10/maria-cruz-acero-guardiana-puig-major-base-militar-cima-mallorca-94458634.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodemallorca.es">[diariodemallorca.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodemallorca.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">November 10, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 10, 2023</span></span></span>

A military radar station, however, does not by itself indicate unexplained activity. Air surveillance units routinely detect, classify and investigate aircraft movements, and the Spanish Ministry of Defence’s later release of UFO files shows that many reported incidents involved normal military or aviation assessment procedures rather than confirmation of extraordinary objects. The declassified archive contains reports of “strange phenomena” investigated by the Spanish Air Force, with conclusions varying from unresolved observations to cases where conventional explanations were considered.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The mountain environment also shaped perception. A high viewpoint, clear Mediterranean skies and long distances across sea and terrain are ideal conditions for watching lights that may be difficult to identify. Aircraft routes, atmospheric effects, satellites, planets and distant lights can appear unusual when viewed without reference points. These factors do not explain every historical report, but they help explain why places such as the Serra de Tramuntana became recurring UFO locations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.arabalears.cat/societat/ovnis-extraterrestres-balears-ufologia-mallorca-puig-major-vedra-tramuntana_1_1181773.amp.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arabalears.cat">[arabalears.cat]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arabalears.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Les Illes, terra d’ovnis?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Les Illes, terra d’ovnis?</span></span></span>

## How publicity shaped the wider sighting wave

Mallorca’s UFO boom grew because sightings were not treated as isolated events. Newspapers, local investigators and enthusiast groups helped connect separate reports into a larger narrative. Areas around Sóller and the Tramuntana mountains became associated with repeated UFO claims, creating what local media later described as a period of “ovnimanía” — a wave of public fascination with unidentified flying objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2023/02/19/1884911/ovnis-mallorca-hace-mas-anos-desato-ovnimania-avistamientos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.es">[Ultima Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ultima HoraOvnis en Mallorca &#124; Hace más de 45 años se desató una ‘ovnimanía&#x27; de avistamientosFebruary 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2023</span></span></span>

One important example of this publicity effect was the continuing attention given to later reports from the same region, including the 1979 Sóller-area incident involving photographer Pep Climent. Stories about unusual lights and alleged objects near the mountains helped reinforce the idea of the Tramuntana as a UFO hotspot, even when individual cases had different levels of evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://productesdemallorca.es/fenomeno-ovni-en-mallorca/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: productesdemallorca.es">[Productes de Mallorca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">productesdemallorca.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Productes de Mallorca Fenómeno OVNI en Mallorca</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Productes de MallorcaFenómeno OVNI en Mallorca - Productes de MallorcaFebruary 25, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 2021</span></span></span>

This pattern is important for understanding Mallorca’s UFO history. The island’s reputation was not built only from strong cases with official documentation. It was also shaped by a feedback loop: a reported sighting attracted media attention; attention encouraged more observers to look at the sky; new reports then strengthened the reputation of the location. Puig Major became famous partly because people were already watching it.

## What the evidence supports — and what remains uncertain

The strongest evidence for Mallorca’s UFO fever is not proof of unusual craft but proof of the social phenomenon itself. The 1978 gathering happened, large numbers of people participated, local organisations promoted sky watching, and the Puig Major area became a recognised centre of UFO interest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ultimahora.es/noticias/local/2023/02/19/1884911/ovnis-mallorca-hace-mas-anos-desato-ovnimania-avistamientos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ultimahora.es">[Ultima Hora]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ultimahora.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ultima HoraOvnis en Mallorca &#124; Hace más de 45 años se desató una ‘ovnimanía&#x27; de avistamientosFebruary 19, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 19, 2023</span></span></span>

The weaker part of the story is the claim that Puig Major was a site of extraterrestrial activity. The surviving record does not provide reliable physical evidence for that conclusion. Reports from the period mainly consist of witness testimony, newspaper accounts and later recollections. Some observations remained unexplained because information was incomplete, but “unidentified” does not mean “alien”.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

For historians of the [Balearic Islands]({{ 'balearic-islands/' | relative_url }})’ UFO record, Puig Major matters because it shows how UFO waves develop. A real place, genuine curiosity, military associations and mass publicity combined to create a powerful local legend. The mountain watches of 1978 were therefore less a solved mystery or a confirmed encounter than a landmark episode in Mallorca’s modern culture of looking for answers in the sky.
