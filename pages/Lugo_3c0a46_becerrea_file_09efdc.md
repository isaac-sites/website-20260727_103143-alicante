## Introduction

The Becerreá UFO file is Lugo’s most important example of an unusual aerial report entering Spain’s official military investigation system. On 2 April 1969, a driver reported seeing a strange object near Becerreá while travelling along the N-VI road, and the Spanish Air Force opened a file that was later declassified. The surviving record is important not because it proves an unknown aircraft, but because it shows how authorities documented, assessed and closed a puzzling report during the Cold War era.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

## The 1969 sighting and military response

The report began on the evening of 2 April 1969, when a lone driver travelling through the Becerreá area described seeing what he believed was an unusual object beside the road. The account later included in the UFO literature described an object with a rounded or elongated shape, estimated by the witness to be several metres long, with a highly unusual illuminated surface. According to later summaries of the case, the witness stopped his vehicle to observe it more closely, after which the object was no longer visible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.becerrea.net/portal/caso_ovni" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: becerrea.net">[Becerreá]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">becerrea.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BecerreáCaso Ovni &#124; Becerrea.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BecerreáCaso Ovni &#124; Becerrea.net</span></span></span>

What made Becerreá unusual within Lugo’s UFO history was not only the description itself, but the administrative path the report followed. The testimony reached Spanish aviation authorities and became part of the files managed by the Air Force’s intelligence structures. The official document was titled *Avistamiento de fenómenos extraños entre Becerreá y Lugo: 02 de Abril de 1969* and was produced by the Air Operational Command’s intelligence section. The six-page file was later declassified in 1993 and published through the Spanish Ministry of Defence’s digital archive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The existence of this file is sometimes misunderstood. A military UFO archive did not represent a list of confirmed extraordinary craft. These documents recorded reports considered worthy of review, together with the investigation and assessment carried out by military personnel. In Becerreá’s case, the archive’s value is historical: it reveals how an official institution handled a civilian report of a possible aerial anomaly.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

## What the archived investigation contains

The surviving file is relatively short but significant because it preserves the original institutional response. It identifies the event, the location, the date and the military body responsible for evaluating it. The record belongs to Spain’s wider collection of declassified UFO files, where reports were catalogued as observations of unidentified phenomena rather than as evidence of extraterrestrial activity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The investigation’s central conclusion was a conventional explanation. According to later reporting on the declassified document, the Air Force assessment suggested that the sighting resulted from an optical effect caused by sunlight reflecting from a granite rock formation. The explanation attempted to match the witness description with a natural visual phenomenon rather than an unknown vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.elprogreso.es/articulo/a-montana/medio-siglo-explicacion/201901082054411353077.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elprogreso.es">[El Progreso de Lugo]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elprogreso.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">El Progreso de Lugo Medio siglo sin explicación</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">El Progreso de Lugo Medio siglo sin explicación</span></span></span>

This conclusion became the main source of later debate. Some investigators argued that the explanation did not convincingly reproduce the witness’s description, particularly because the reported object was described as having a specific shape and appearance rather than simply being a bright reflection. Critics of the official interpretation also questioned whether the investigation had enough field information to rule out other possibilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.becerrea.net/portal/caso_ovni" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: becerrea.net">[Becerreá]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">becerrea.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">BecerreáCaso Ovni &#124; Becerrea.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BecerreáCaso Ovni &#124; Becerrea.net</span></span></span>

At the same time, the limits of the case must be recognised. The archive does not contain evidence that the object was a technological craft. The witness account is the core of the case, and no independent measurements or physical traces are available. The disagreement is therefore not between proof and disproof, but between different interpretations of a limited set of observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

## Why the case remains disputed

The Becerreá file has lasted in UFO discussions because it contains two elements that rarely appear together: an official investigation and an unresolved argument over the explanation. The military involvement gives the case a stronger documentary foundation than many ordinary sightings, but it does not automatically make the original claim stronger. Official attention shows that a report was considered worth examining; it does not establish what caused the observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The strongest evidence in favour of taking the case seriously is the existence of a contemporary record. The report was not created decades later from memory alone; it entered an official archive soon after the event and was preserved as part of Spain’s Air Force UFO documentation. This makes Becerreá a valuable historical source for understanding how unusual sightings were processed by authorities in 1960s Spain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The strongest evidence against extraordinary interpretations is the lack of corroboration. There are no known photographs, recovered objects, radar traces, aircraft records showing an unknown vehicle, or multiple independent witnesses observing the same event. Without those forms of evidence, the case cannot move beyond the category of an unexplained report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?id=38113" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

A further complication is that later researchers and journalists approached the file from different perspectives. Some focused on perceived weaknesses in the official explanation, while others emphasised that unusual perceptions can have ordinary causes, especially when a single observer views an object briefly from a moving vehicle.
