## Introduction

[Zamora]({{ 'zamoras-ufo-stories-beyond-the/' | relative_url }})’s UFO archive was not built around a single official investigation or a confirmed discovery. It developed through decades of newspaper reports, witness accounts, private research, and efforts by local investigators to organise scattered stories into a historical record. The most significant recent attempt to bring this material together is *Ufología histórica de Zamora* by Nando Domínguez, a compilation of reported sightings and related documentation covering material from the mid-20th century onwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.editorialguanteblanco.com/publicaciones/ufologia-historica-de-zamora/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: editorialguanteblanco.com">[Guante Blanco]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">editorialguanteblanco.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guante BlancoUfología histórica de Zamora¿Sabías que Zamora es una zona alta de avistamientos ovni? The value of Zamora’s UFO archive is therefore historical rather than evidential in the strict scientific sense. It shows how reports emerged, how witnesses described unusual experiences, how newspapers shaped public interest, and how later researchers revisited older cases. It preserves claims and investigations, but it does not by itself prove that unexplained sightings had extraordinary causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.editorialguanteblanco.com/publicaciones/ufologia-historica-de-zamora/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: editorialguanteblanco.com">[Guante Blanco]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">editorialguanteblanco.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guante BlancoUfología histórica de Zamora¿Sabías que Zamora es una zona alta de avistamientos ovni? ## Local investigators and collections

The backbone of Zamora’s UFO record has been the work of local researchers who treated scattered reports as material for a provincial history. Rather than relying only on famous cases, these investigators have searched through newspaper archives, collected witness memories, and attempted to place individual reports into a wider timeline.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.editorialguanteblanco.com/publicaciones/ufologia-historica-de-zamora/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: editorialguanteblanco.com">[Guante Blanco]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">editorialguanteblanco.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guante BlancoUfología histórica de Zamora¿Sabías que Zamora es una zona alta de avistamientos ovni? Si eres de los que le gustan los buen...</span></span></span>

The best-known modern figure in this process is Nando Domínguez, a Zamora-born researcher and writer whose book *Ufología histórica de Zamora* was published by Editorial Guante Blanco in 2022. The project gathered reports from newspapers and other records, presenting decades of alleged sightings, unusual aerial observations, and local UFO stories as a chronological archive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.editorialguanteblanco.com/publicaciones/ufologia-historica-de-zamora/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: editorialguanteblanco.com">[Guante Blanco]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">editorialguanteblanco.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guante BlancoUfología histórica de Zamora¿Sabías que Zamora es una zona alta de avistamientos ovni? Domínguez’s approach reflects a common pattern in regional UFO research: the investigator acts partly as an archivist. The main task is often not proving what happened in the sky, but preserving information that might otherwise disappear. Newspaper articles may contain the first public description of an event, while later interviews can add details that were not recorded at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://usercontent.one/wp/www.ovnispain.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Ufologia-historica-de-Zamora-El-Dia-de-Zamora-Periodico-n408.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usercontent.one">[Usercontent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usercontent.one</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufologia historica de Zamora El Dia de Zamora Periodico n408</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EL DÍA DE TORO 131 Dec 2022 — Nando Domínguez, autor de &quot;Ufología histórica&quot;, &quot;una recopilación de todos los casos publicados...</span></span></span>

A related project has been the creation of an interactive map of reported UFO sightings in Zamora. This type of tool changes the way a local archive can be viewed, allowing reports to be connected to locations rather than remaining as isolated stories in books or newspaper files.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.elespanol.com/castilla-y-leon/region/zamora/20240110/mapa-interactivo-recoge-avistamientos-ovnis-detectados-provincia-zamora/823917970_0.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elespanol.com">[EL ESPAÑOL]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elespanol.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EL ESPAÑOLUn mapa interactivo recoge los avistamientos de ovnis</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 10, 2024 — 10 Jan 2024 — El escritor y ufólogo zamorano, Nando Domínguez, ha lanzado un mapa interactivo que detalla casos de avi...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 10, 2024</span></span></span>

However, local investigation also creates challenges. Researchers working with historical reports often depend on incomplete information: a newspaper summary may not identify all witnesses, memories may change over time, and original documents may no longer exist. The archive can show that a report was made, but not necessarily that the reported object was correctly identified or physically verified.

## Newspaper records and witness interviews

For Zamora, newspapers are among the most important sources because many UFO reports entered public awareness through local journalism. Reports from villages and rural areas were often recorded because witnesses contacted newspapers after seeing unusual lights or objects. These articles provide dates, locations, witness names in some cases, and descriptions of what people believed they had seen.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://usercontent.one/wp/www.ovnispain.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Ufologia-historica-de-Zamora-El-Dia-de-Zamora-Periodico-n408.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usercontent.one">[Usercontent]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usercontent.one</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufologia historica de Zamora El Dia de Zamora Periodico n408</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EL DÍA DE TORO 131 Dec 2022 — Nando Domínguez, autor de &quot;Ufología histórica&quot;, &quot;una recopilación de todos los casos publicados...</span></span></span>

This type of material is valuable to historians because it preserves the social history of sightings. A newspaper report can reveal how a community reacted, whether an event attracted attention, and how explanations developed afterwards. For example, a mysterious light might first appear as an unexplained object and later be reconsidered as an aircraft, astronomical phenomenon, or other conventional source.

The archive work surrounding Zamora’s cases has also highlighted the importance of returning to witnesses years after an event. Follow-up interviews can recover personal memories, but they also introduce a problem familiar to researchers in many fields: human memory is not a recording device. Later conversations may add details, but they can also be influenced by media coverage, previous discussions, or expectations about what happened.

This is why a careful archive separates different layers of information:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Contemporary records:** what newspapers or official documents reported close to the date of the event.
* **Witness testimony:** what people said they experienced, either at the time or years later.
* **Later interpretation:** explanations offered by investigators, sceptics, or UFO researchers.

</div>

Keeping these categories separate is essential when assessing Zamora’s UFO history.

## The difference between reports and proof

The central question in Zamora’s archive is not whether reports exist. They clearly do. The more difficult question is what those reports demonstrate.

A documented sighting proves that someone reported seeing something unusual. It does not automatically prove that the object was unknown, technological, or non-human. This distinction is especially important because many UFO archives around the world contain a mixture of cases: some remain unexplained because information is missing, some are misidentifications, and some become more understandable after additional investigation.

Spain’s own official UFO archive provides a useful comparison. The Spanish Ministry of Defence has released 80 files containing around 1,900 pages of reports involving unusual aerial observations connected in some way with Air Force personnel or equipment. The release of these files shows that governments have investigated unusual sightings, but the existence of an official file does not mean the event was confirmed as extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/micrositios/inicio.do" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Biblioteca Virtual Defensa Expedientes OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Biblioteca Virtual DefensaExpedientes OVNI - Biblioteca Virtual de DefensaSe trata de un total de 80 expedientes, 1.900 páginas de avista...</span></span></span>

Zamora’s private archive works differently. It is primarily a collection of civilian reports rather than a military investigation programme. That makes it valuable for understanding public experience and local culture, but it also means many cases lack the types of evidence that would allow stronger conclusions, such as radar records, photographs with reliable provenance, instrument measurements, or independent observers.
