## Introduction

On 20 February 1997, 76-year-old livestock keeper Heliodoro Núñez reported seeing two enormous, brightly illuminated figures in a meadow near Paradaseca, in the [Ourense]({{ 'ourense-ufo-history-between-legend-and/' | relative_url }}) municipality of Chandrexa de Queixa. He said the figures stood partly concealed behind vegetation and an oak tree, appeared to be about three metres tall, and remained visible for roughly twenty minutes. Frightened, he prayed and then ran home, repeatedly looking back and seeing the forms still in place.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Ourense_35c272_paradaseca_luminous_1093d5-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Paradaseca" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The case matters because it is one of Ourense’s most vivid close-encounter stories, yet its evidential foundation is much narrower than later paranormal retellings sometimes suggest. Núñez’s distress appears to have been genuine, and another local man reportedly noticed an unusual red light in the district. Nevertheless, nobody independently confirmed the giant figures, no physical traces were recorded and no official investigation established what had been present. The most defensible conclusion is therefore not that Núñez invented the experience, but that he sincerely misidentified an ambiguous illuminated object or arrangement in the landscape. An unknown encounter cannot be ruled out absolutely, but the surviving evidence does not require one.

## The reported figures near the tree

Núñez was tending livestock at about four in the afternoon beside the River Devesas, around three kilometres from the turning towards Chandrexa. According to the contemporary newspaper account, he saw two large, heavily built figures close together behind an oak. He estimated their height at approximately three metres and described them as apparently sharing a single cone-shaped head. Their outlines were surrounded by lights and coloured flashes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span>

Later versions add that his dogs began barking towards the location, causing him initially to suspect wolves. The forms were said to project above bushes and to resemble conjoined beings wearing a luminous mitre or pointed headdress. Their colours allegedly shifted through red, blue, yellow and green. These details came from later interviews by UFO enthusiasts rather than solely from the brief newspaper report, so they should not automatically be treated as equally secure parts of the original observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com/2012/08/galicia-encuentro-con-humanoides.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com">[elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span></span></span>

The duration is one of the case’s strangest features. Núñez reportedly watched for about twenty minutes before fleeing. That is much longer than a meteor, flash of lightning or rapidly passing aircraft would remain visible. It also makes a fleeting hallucination less persuasive as a complete explanation. However, a long observation does not guarantee accurate identification. A fixed object viewed through branches, partly hidden vegetation or changing reflected light can remain baffling precisely because it does not move into a more recognisable position.

His estimate of three metres also deserves caution. Apparent size depends on knowing distance. If an observer cannot tell whether something is a small nearby object or a larger form farther away, its true dimensions cannot be calculated reliably. Here, the figures were partly screened by a tree and bushes, and there is no surviving measured distance, photograph, map or reconstruction of Núñez’s exact viewpoint. The height was therefore an impression, not a measurement.

Núñez’s behaviour after the sighting supports the sincerity of his fear more strongly than it supports any particular interpretation. A neighbour, Armando Portal, reportedly saw him running and shouting, while villagers described Núñez and his family as ordinary people not known for making foolish claims. Núñez himself did not insist that he had identified extraterrestrials; he repeatedly said that he did not know what the objects were and did not want to experience anything similar again.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span>

That restraint is important. It separates the witness’s actual position — fear and uncertainty — from the more definite “humanoid encounter” language applied by later writers.

## Corroboration and later retellings

The strongest corroboration is limited to Núñez’s emotional state. Portal witnessed his frightened flight, and Núñez’s wife later reportedly said that he returned home deeply shaken, prayed repeatedly, slept badly and struggled to eat over the following days. Such reactions make a casual joke less likely, although they cannot establish what produced the fear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span>

A more intriguing secondary claim concerns Juan González González, a resident of nearby Casteligo. In a later published retelling, González said that at approximately the same time he noticed a stationary, intense red light above the trees. He reportedly considered fire, machinery and a balloon but rejected each possibility because there was no smoke and the light did not appear to move. He did not see the supposed giants, although he said he encountered Núñez running with his animals and looking frightened.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com/2012/08/galicia-encuentro-con-humanoides.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com">[elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span></span></span>

This account modestly strengthens the possibility that a real light source was present in the area. It does not, however, independently confirm beings. The two men may have viewed the same object from different positions, or they may have seen unrelated things. The surviving material does not provide bearings, distances or a site survey that could demonstrate whether González’s red light aligned with Núñez’s figures.

The evidence is further complicated by differences between accounts. The contemporary archive describes figures behind an oak that still carried leaves, surrounded by coloured flashes and apparently joined beneath one cone-shaped head. Later versions introduce barking dogs, bushes, a bishop-like hat, a clearer sequence of changing colours and investigative visits by UFO groups.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span>

Additional detail is not necessarily false: follow-up interviews often recover information omitted from short newspaper reports. But memory can also become reorganised through repeated questioning and retelling. Once a case is framed as an encounter with “humanoids”, uncertain shapes may acquire anatomy, clothing and intentions that were less definite during the original experience.

No accessible account documents photographs of the object, footprints, damaged vegetation, radiation measurements, recovered material, police attendance or a formal inquiry by Spain’s military UFO investigators. Nor is there evidence of radar or aircraft activity associated with the sighting. This leaves the case dependent on testimony and later narrative development rather than independently testable records.

## Mistaken perception or unknown encounter

Several ordinary explanations remain possible, although none can now be demonstrated conclusively.

**A light or reflective object partly hidden by vegetation** is the most economical possibility. A sheet, agricultural material, machinery component, plastic covering or other object could have reflected sunlight or another strong light source through moving branches. Two bright vertical areas separated by a trunk or shadow might have appeared as paired figures, while the upper branches or a pointed object created the impression of a shared conical head.

Changes between red, blue, yellow and green could have resulted from light passing through foliage, atmospheric moisture, reflective surfaces or small movements by the observer. Human colour perception becomes especially unreliable when viewing intense or dazzling light against a darker background. González’s reported red glow, if accurately recalled, is consistent with some real illuminated or reflective source being present, but not specifically with a living entity.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com/2012/08/galicia-encuentro-con-humanoides.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com">[elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elcalizdejudas.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">POZ O DE ALMAS: GALICIA: ¿ ENCUENTRO CON HUMANOIDES?</span></span></span>

**Pareidolia and threat perception** offer a mechanism for turning an unclear scene into figures. Pareidolia is the normal tendency to detect meaningful forms, especially faces and bodies, in ambiguous patterns. Experimental research shows that the brain identifies face-like configurations rapidly, sometimes before the underlying object has been properly recognised. Trees, shadows and everyday objects are common triggers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://amandakrobinson.github.io/pdfs/Stuart_etal_2025_oneobjecttwoidentities_JEPHPP.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: amandakrobinson.github.io">[amandakrobinson.github.io]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">amandakrobinson.github.io</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Stuart etal 2025 oneobjecttwoidentities JEPHPP</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance 2025 American Psychological Association ISSN: 0096-1523 2025, Vol. 5...</span></span></span>

Once Núñez interpreted the shapes as beings, fear may have stabilised that reading. The barking dogs, unfamiliar lights and isolated setting would have encouraged a threat-based interpretation. Looking back while running would not necessarily have corrected the mistake: brief, anxious glances from increasing distance could preserve the same overall silhouette while revealing less detail.

**People, equipment or a prank** cannot be excluded, but the reported scale and difficult access make a simple hoax less convincing. Núñez argued that the location was not easy to reach, and no pranksters or equipment were identified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.laregion.es/comarca-de-ourense/hemeroteca-avistamiento-extraterrestres-paradaseca_1_20220224-2278366.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: laregion.es">[La Región]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">laregion.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La Región Hemeroteca &#124; ¿Un avistamiento de extraterrestres en Paradaseca?</span></span></span> Yet remoteness is not proof that nobody could have placed an object there, and an ordinary item need not have been positioned deliberately to deceive him.

**A medical or hallucinatory episode** is possible but poorly supported for the meadow sighting. An unusual psychological or neurological event also cannot be diagnosed from a newspaper story. There is no medical history, vision assessment or evidence of confusion before or after the incident. Treating hallucination as established would be as unjustified as treating extraterrestrial visitors as established.

## How strong is the Paradaseca case?

The Paradaseca incident is best described as a sincere but weakly evidenced unidentified experience. Its strongest features are the named witness, the rapid local reporting, the witness’s evident distress and the separate report of a red light in roughly the same district and time window. Its principal weaknesses are more serious: only Núñez saw the figures; their distance and size were not measured; descriptions became more elaborate in later accounts; and no photograph, trace, instrument record or official case file has emerged.

The red-light testimony prevents the episode from being dismissed simply as an invented story. It leaves open the possibility that Núñez encountered a genuinely unusual visual stimulus. But “unusual” is not the same as extraterrestrial or paranormal. The available material cannot distinguish confidently between reflected light, agricultural equipment, an unfamiliar object, distorted vegetation, people at an uncertain distance or a rarer perceptual event.

Within Ourense’s UFO history, Paradaseca is memorable less because it proves a close encounter than because it shows how a powerful local mystery can grow from an honest witness, a confusing landscape and incomplete documentation. Heliodoro Núñez appears never to have found a satisfying answer to what frightened him. More than three decades later, the evidence permits several plausible explanations but does not preserve enough detail to select one with confidence.
