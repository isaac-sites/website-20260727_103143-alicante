## Why Reus became Tarragona’s key UFO location

### The 1969 Reus Air Base sighting

The central event in Tarragona’s UFO record occurred at the former Reus military airfield, now associated with Reus Airport. At around midday on 13 May 1969, a controller watching a Boeing 727 climb after take-off noticed a bright, apparently stationary object near the aircraft’s flight path. Other personnel, including another controller, radio mechanics and soldiers, also reported seeing it. ### The official explanation: an unusual optical effect

The [Reus case]({{ 'reus-case/' | relative_url }}) did not end with a confirmed unknown object. The investigation considered several possibilities and concluded that a likely explanation was an optical phenomenon caused by atmospheric conditions, sunlight and the position of aircraft in relation to the observer. The report suggested a refraction effect involving aircraft and the nearly vertical position of the Sun could have produced the appearance of a bright stationary object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diaridetarragona.com/reus/1526/el-ejercito-ha-investigado-cuatro-fenomenos-ovni-en-la-provincia-20160213-0017-emdt201602130017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diaridetarragona.com">[Diari de Tarragona]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diaridetarragona.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diari de TarragonaEl Ejército ha investigado cuatro &#x27;fenómenos OVNI&#x27; en la provinciaMay 19, 2017...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 19, 2017</span></span></span>

This conclusion illustrates an important point in UFO investigations: an observation can be genuine without the original interpretation being correct. The witnesses may have accurately described what they saw, while the cause remained uncertain until environmental and technical factors were considered.

## Why aviation shaped Tarragona’s UFO history

Tarragona’s strongest UFO stories are closely connected with aviation infrastructure rather than remote locations or folklore. Reus was an active military and civil aviation site, making it a place where unusual observations were more likely to be noticed and formally recorded.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/cat/2016/11/11/catalunya/1478881679_067169.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

Airfields also create conditions that can complicate sightings:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* aircraft lights can be mistaken for stationary objects when viewed from certain angles;
* reflections from aircraft surfaces can appear detached from their source;
* altitude and distance can make ordinary objects difficult to judge;
* controllers and pilots may observe unfamiliar events but still lack enough information for immediate identification.

</div>

This does not mean every aviation-related UFO report is easily explained. It means the setting provides both better documentation and more opportunities for confusion.

## What the evidence does — and does not — show

### Evidence supporting a serious investigation

Tarragona’s strongest cases deserve attention because they were not simply stories repeated decades later. The Reus incident produced an official report, involved several witnesses and was examined by military authorities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?control=BMDB20160068825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

The existence of a file proves that something was reported and investigated. It does not, by itself, prove that the reported object was extraordinary.

### The main doubts and limitations

The biggest weakness in Tarragona’s UFO record is the lack of physical evidence. There are no publicly available materials, recovered objects or independent measurements demonstrating that any reported sighting involved technology beyond known aviation, astronomical or atmospheric explanations.

Many historical UFO cases rely heavily on perception: estimating distance, speed, size and movement in the sky is extremely difficult without instruments. Even experienced observers can be misled when an object lacks a clear reference point.

The Reus investigation is therefore best understood as an unresolved historical observation that was given a plausible conventional explanation, rather than as evidence of an unknown craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://reusdigital.cat/noticies/reus/els-expedients-ovni-de-reus" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reusdigital.cat">[Reus Digital]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reusdigital.cat</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on reusdigital.cat.</span></span></span>

## Tarragona’s place in Spanish UFO history

Tarragona is a small but valuable part of Spain’s broader UFO archive. Across Spain, the Air Force collected and later released dozens of reports from the Cold War era, documenting encounters that ranged from unexplained lights to possible aircraft or atmospheric phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://english.elpais.com/cat/2016/11/11/catalunya/1478881679_067169.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: english.elpais.com">[EL PAÍS English]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">english.elpais.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elpais.com.</span></span></span>

Within that wider history, Tarragona stands out mainly because of the Reus case: a well-recorded aviation incident that sits between two interpretations. For UFO enthusiasts, it remains an intriguing example of a sighting that involved official witnesses. For sceptical investigators, it demonstrates how careful examination can reduce a mysterious report to a likely optical explanation.

The lasting importance of Tarragona’s UFO history is therefore not that it proves extraordinary events occurred, but that it shows how unusual sightings are recorded, investigated and reassessed over time.
