## Introduction

The 1968 interception over [Lleida]({{ 'why-lleidas-best-ufo-cases-look-less/' | relative_url }}) remains one of the province’s most discussed UFO cases because it involved more than a single witness: military pilots saw the object, radar tracked it, and fighter aircraft were sent to investigate. On 17 May 1968, Spanish Air Force crews reported a metallic object at extreme altitude above Lleida, but the evidence did not show the rapid manoeuvres or unusual flight characteristics often associated with stronger UFO claims. The later official interpretation was that the target was most likely a French scientific balloon, a conclusion based largely on its altitude, slow movement and the timing of high-altitude balloon activity in the region.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lleida.com/sites/default/files/u4626/1968-05-17_avistamiento_en_lerida.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lleida.com">[Lleida]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lleida.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 05 17 avistamiento en lerida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOSApril 6, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 6, 2016</span></span></span>

The case is therefore important not because it proves an unknown craft, but because it shows how a dramatic military encounter can move from “unidentified” to a plausible conventional explanation. It is one of Lleida’s strongest UFO stories precisely because the investigation left a detailed trail of observations, while also showing the limits of pilot and radar evidence when an object’s true nature is unclear.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lleida.com/sites/default/files/u4626/1968-05-17_avistamiento_en_lerida.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lleida.com">[Lleida]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lleida.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 05 17 avistamiento en lerida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOSApril 6, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 6, 2016</span></span></span>

## What the pilots and radar recorded

The incident began at around 10:00 local time on 17 May 1968. Two Spanish Air Force F-86 Sabre pilots involved in an operation reported seeing an apparently stationary metallic object roughly over Lleida. Their description was of two apparently superimposed bodies, with a shaped lower section. The nearby EVA-1 radar unit also detected a target at around 76,000 feet (about 23 kilometres) and reported that it appeared to be slowly rising.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lleida.com/noticia_canal/shan-albirat-ovnis-en-territori-lleidata" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lleida.com">[Lleida]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lleida.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">S&#x27;han albirat ovnis en territori lleidatà?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">S&#x27;han albirat ovnis en territori lleidatà?July 7, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 7, 2021</span></span></span>

The response was unusually direct. The F-86 aircraft were instructed to approach and identify the object, but they could not get close enough before fuel limitations forced them to withdraw. A second attempt followed using two F-104 Starfighters equipped with cameras. The pilots reached visual contact and described changing impressions of the object, including comparisons to an inverted spearhead, a squid-like shape and something resembling an aircraft suspended vertically.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>

The F-104s climbed to approximately 59,000 feet, but the object was still estimated to be about seven kilometres higher. The altitude gap made a close approach and photography extremely difficult. A camera attempt was made under poor conditions, but the resulting film was unusable because it had been exposed or damaged. Radar contact reportedly continued until the evening, when the target was lost at an altitude of about 83,000 feet.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>

These details explain why the case gained attention. It was not simply a distant light reported from the ground: trained observers, military equipment and aircraft interception were all involved. However, those factors establish that an unusual aerial target was observed; they do not by themselves identify what the object was. The central question became whether its behaviour matched an unknown aircraft or something much more ordinary.

## Why the interception failed

The failure to intercept the object was one of the features that made the case memorable. To observers at the time, the involvement of fast fighter aircraft suggested a serious unidentified target. Yet the mechanics of the encounter point in a different direction: the fighters were operating near their practical limits while the object remained at an exceptionally high altitude.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>

The military report’s recorded movement was also significant. The object was described as moving slowly horizontally, at roughly 20–25 kilometres per hour, with a vertical movement of only a few metres per minute. Those figures did not suggest a powered aircraft capable of rapid acceleration or sudden manoeuvres. Instead, they were consistent with an object drifting in the upper atmosphere.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>

The radar evidence also requires careful interpretation. The report noted two close radar echoes rather than a single clearly identified aircraft. A large balloon with attached equipment could potentially create multiple returns, especially when viewed from long distances and at unusual angles. The radar confirmed the presence of something in the sky, but it did not provide a definitive identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>

This distinction is central to understanding the case. Military sensors are valuable because they provide measurements beyond human eyesight, but a radar contact is not automatically evidence of an extraordinary object. The quality of the conclusion depends on whether the recorded speed, altitude, shape and movement fit known possibilities.

## How the French balloon theory fits

The later explanation linked the Lleida sighting to French high-altitude scientific balloon activity. The Spanish Air Force’s assessment connected the May 1968 reports with a period when large French stratospheric balloons were operating, and it classified the case as a French CNES balloon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elojocritico.info/los-archivos-ovni-del-ejercito-del-aire-desglosados-quien-que-como/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elojocritico.info">[elojocritico.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elojocritico.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on elojocritico.info.</span></span></span>

The balloon explanation fits several of the most unusual features of the encounter:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Extreme altitude:** Scientific balloons were designed to operate far above normal aircraft. CNES has operated scientific balloon programmes reaching the stratosphere for atmospheric and scientific research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cnes.fr/en/projects/balloons" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnes.fr">[CNES]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnes.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Balloons &#124; CNES</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Balloons &#124; CNES...</span></span></span>
* **Slow movement:** The reported speed and gradual ascent match a drifting balloon more closely than a conventional aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.scribd.com/document/328831642/Avistamiento-de-OVNI-en-Lleida" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: es.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">es.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avistamiento de OVNI en Lleida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avistamiento OVNI en Lérida, 1968 &#124; PDF &#124; Aviación &#124; Aeronáutica...</span></span></span>
* **Changing appearance:** A balloon, suspended equipment and reflective surfaces can appear as different shapes depending on viewing angle, distance and lighting conditions.

## What remains uncertain about the case

The balloon explanation is strong, but some details are less complete than enthusiasts sometimes assume. The publicly available documentation does not provide a full reconstruction with every technical detail, such as a complete flight path matching the object minute by minute. The identification rests on the overall fit between the observations and the known balloon activity rather than on a recovered object or photographic proof.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lleida.com/sites/default/files/u4626/1968-05-17_avistamiento_en_lerida.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lleida.com">[Lleida]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lleida.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 05 17 avistamiento en lerida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOSApril 6, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 6, 2016</span></span></span>

## Lleida’s strongest interception, but not its strongest mystery

Within Lleida’s UFO history, the 1968 interception stands apart because it combined aviation, radar and official investigation. It was not dismissed as a simple misunderstanding at the time; the Spanish Air Force treated it seriously enough to scramble aircraft and create a formal file.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lleida.com/sites/default/files/u4626/1968-05-17_avistamiento_en_lerida.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lleida.com">[Lleida]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lleida.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1968 05 17 avistamiento en lerida</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AVISTAMIENTO DE FENOMENOS EXTRAÑOSApril 6, 2016...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 6, 2016</span></span></span>
