## Introduction

The Granja de Moreruela encounter is one of the most distinctive UFO stories associated with [Zamora]({{ 'zamoras-ufo-stories-beyond-the/' | relative_url }}) because it is not simply a report of an unusual light in the sky. The case concerns an alleged close encounter on 28 May 1974 in which Guillermo Rodríguez Riesco and his cousin reportedly saw a humanoid figure near the River Esla, close to Granja de Moreruela. Later accounts described a figure wearing a close-fitting outfit and a helmet-like covering, followed by an apparent levitation or disappearance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zamoranews.com/articulo/reportajes/sombras-historias-escenarios-paranormales-que-resguarda-zamora/20240121192630271042.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zamoranews.com">[Zamora News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zamoranews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zamora NewsEntre sombras e historias: los escenarios paranormales que resguarda ZamoraJanuary 21, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2024</span></span></span>

## The 1974 encounter and witness accounts

According to later investigations, Guillermo Rodríguez Riesco, known as “Willy”, was fishing near the River Esla area of Granja de Moreruela when the encounter allegedly occurred. Accounts state that he was accompanied by his cousin and dogs when they noticed an unusual figure. The reported being was described as humanoid, with clothing interpreted as a tight suit and a head covering resembling a helmet or diving apparatus. The story later gained attention because the witness claimed the figure reacted to their approach and then appeared to rise or vanish.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zamoranews.com/articulo/reportajes/sombras-historias-escenarios-paranormales-que-resguarda-zamora/20240121192630271042.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zamoranews.com">[Zamora News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zamoranews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zamora NewsEntre sombras e historias: los escenarios paranormales que resguarda ZamoraJanuary 21, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2024</span></span></span>

The setting contributed to the case’s lasting appeal. Unlike many UFO reports involving distant lights, this was presented as a direct encounter in a familiar rural environment. The witness was not describing something seen through binoculars or across the night sky, but an alleged face-to-face experience at ground level. This placed the case in the category often called a “close encounter” within UFO literature, where witness perception and memory become especially important because there is usually little physical evidence available for later examination.

The story was later circulated through Spanish UFO publications and local investigations. One influential source was Federico Acosta Noriega’s book *Ovnis sobre Zamora*, which collected UFO reports from the province and helped preserve the Moreruela account in UFO literature. Acosta Noriega, a Zamora-based writer and former judge, became one of the best-known local figures associated with documenting these reports during the 1970s.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Acosta_Noriega" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Federico Acosta Noriega</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Federico Acosta Noriega</span></span></span>

## Investigations and the missing physical evidence

The main strength of the Granja de Moreruela case is the existence of a specific witness narrative that has remained consistent enough to be repeatedly discussed by later researchers. Local investigators, including Nando Domínguez in more recent years, have revisited the story and interviewed Rodríguez Riesco, helping keep the account in circulation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zamoranews.com/articulo/reportajes/sombras-historias-escenarios-paranormales-que-resguarda-zamora/20240121192630271042.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zamoranews.com">[Zamora News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zamoranews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zamora NewsEntre sombras e historias: los escenarios paranormales que resguarda ZamoraJanuary 21, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2024</span></span></span>

The main weakness is that the case lacks the kinds of evidence that would allow independent verification. There are no publicly available photographs of the alleged entity, no confirmed physical traces from the location, no recorded measurements, and no independent observers who can confirm the central event. Later retellings depend heavily on interviews, books, media reports, and UFO-focused publications rather than contemporaneous scientific or official documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bitacora.ricardomartin.info/category/zamora/page/5/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bitacora.ricardomartin.info">[Bitácora de Ricardo Martín]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bitacora.ricardomartin.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bitácora de Ricardo Martínrmbit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La bitácora personal de Ricardo Martín...</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because a credible witness account and evidence of an extraordinary event are not the same thing. A person may sincerely report an unusual experience while the cause remains uncertain. In close-encounter cases, investigators generally look for additional support such as multiple independent witnesses, recordings, physical effects, or documentation made close to the time of the event. Those elements are largely absent in the Moreruela case.

The investigation history also reflects the wider pattern of Spanish UFO research in the 1970s. Many reports were collected by private researchers and journalists rather than through controlled scientific investigation. This preserved valuable historical testimony but often left unanswered questions about timing, original notes, witness interviews, environmental conditions, and alternative explanations.

## Why the story remains unresolved

The continuing debate around Granja de Moreruela comes from the gap between a vivid claim and limited evidence. Supporters of the case point to the detail of the witness description, the unusual nature of the alleged encounter, and the fact that the story has been repeated for decades without a clear conventional explanation emerging.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.zamoranews.com/articulo/reportajes/sombras-historias-escenarios-paranormales-que-resguarda-zamora/20240121192630271042.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: zamoranews.com">[Zamora News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">zamoranews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zamora NewsEntre sombras e historias: los escenarios paranormales que resguarda ZamoraJanuary 21, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2024</span></span></span>

Sceptical interpretations focus on the absence of independent confirmation. A single extraordinary perception, recorded mainly through later accounts, cannot by itself establish that the event involved an unknown technology or a non-human presence. Possible explanations in cases of this type can include misidentification, unusual human activity, memory reconstruction over time, or later embellishment as stories are retold.

The case is therefore best understood as an important piece of Zamora’s UFO folklore and a notable example of Spain’s 1970s close-encounter reports. It shows why some UFO cases remain culturally significant even when they do not produce decisive evidence.     var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
