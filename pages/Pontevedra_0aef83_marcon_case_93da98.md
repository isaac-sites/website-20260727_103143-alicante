## Introduction

The Marcón UFO story is one of [Pontevedra]({{ 'pontevedras-ufo-cases-between-mystery/' | relative_url }})’s most famous modern UFO reports, but its importance comes from the history of the claim rather than from confirmed evidence. According to accounts published decades later, residents of Marcón, a parish of Pontevedra, reported seeing an unusual circular object on 26 March 1982, followed by claims of a disturbed area on nearby land. The episode became a lasting part of local UFO folklore because it combined multiple witness accounts, newspaper attention and later investigation. However, no publicly verified physical evidence, scientific analysis or official record has established that the event involved an extraterrestrial craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

## The 1982 reports from Marcón

The reported event took place on the afternoon of 26 March 1982. Later accounts describe several residents of Marcón observing what they interpreted as a circular object near Monte Carrasco. The reports claimed that the object appeared after a loud explosion-like sound, descended towards the area, disturbed vegetation and then departed at high speed. Descriptions of the object varied in colour and appearance, with later retellings presenting it as a large circular craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

The story attracted attention because it was not presented only as a distant light in the sky. Later investigators focused on alleged effects at the location where the object was said to have landed. According to accounts collected by UFO researchers, a local science teacher, Francisco Javier Rivas, inspected the area and reported changes to the ground, including a patch described as having altered vegetation and several cylindrical holes. Later visitors claimed to have taken soil samples and found the site still changed years afterwards.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

These claims helped give Marcón a stronger place in UFO literature than many ordinary sightings. A report involving an alleged physical trace naturally attracted more attention than a simple observation of lights. However, the key limitation is that the claimed traces were not preserved through a documented scientific investigation that could independently confirm their origin. The available record is mainly made up of witness testimony, journalistic accounts and later investigation by UFO enthusiasts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

### The witnesses and the original account

The credibility of Marcón largely rests on the people who reported seeing something unusual. Later accounts name several witnesses, including residents who maintained their descriptions years afterwards. The continued consistency of some testimonies is often cited by supporters of the case as a reason to take the report seriously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

However, consistent testimony does not automatically identify the cause of an event. A witness can accurately describe what they experienced while still being mistaken about what produced it. This distinction is central to evaluating UFO cases: a reliable person may report a genuine observation, but the interpretation of that observation can remain uncertain.

## How newspapers and researchers retold the case

Marcón became significant partly because it moved beyond a short-lived local rumour. Contemporary coverage by local media helped record the story, while later researchers returned to the case and expanded its reputation. The involvement of UFO investigators, including Ángel Carracelas, turned the event into a reference point in discussions of Galician UFO reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

The later retellings also changed the way the case was remembered. Early reports focused on what residents said they saw; later accounts placed more emphasis on the alleged landing site, the supposed marks on the ground and questions about what could have caused them. This process is common in famous UFO cases: each retelling can preserve details, but it can also make it harder to separate the original observation from later interpretation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

Marcón’s reputation was also reinforced by its place within Pontevedra’s wider UFO history. It became one of the province’s signature cases because it appeared to offer more than a visual sighting: witnesses claimed an encounter with a physical location and possible traces. Yet the absence of publicly available laboratory studies, official investigations or independently verified samples means that the most dramatic elements remain claims rather than established facts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diariodepontevedra.es/blog/rodrigo-cota-galicia-histerica/el-ovni-de-marcon/201903232005191028028.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diariodepontevedra.es">[Diario de Pontevedra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diariodepontevedra.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Diario de Pontevedra El ovni de Marcón</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Diario de PontevedraEl ovni de MarcónMarch 30, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why the evidence remains disputed
