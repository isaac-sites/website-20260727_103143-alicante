## Introduction

The Turís encounter is one of the most discussed close-encounter reports in [Valencia]({{ 'valencias-ufo-cases-between-mystery-and/' | relative_url }})’s UFO history because it rests on an unusually detailed but highly personal witness account. On 25 July 1979, farmer Federico Ibáñez reported seeing a strange object on a rural road near Turís, followed by a close observation of small humanoid figures before the object departed. The case became important not because it produced independent physical proof, but because investigators carried out unusually extensive interviews and field checks soon after the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39622667/IMAGINACION_O_REALIDAD_EL_ATERRIZAJE_DE_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">imaginacion o realidad. el aterrizaje de turisUn amplio informe de investigación se publicó en nuestro libro Los OVNIS y la Cienc...</span></span></span>

The central dispute around Turís is therefore not whether Ibáñez reported a dramatic experience — he clearly did — but how much confidence should be placed in a single witness narrative describing an extraordinary event. Supporters have pointed to the consistency of his account and the effort made to document the scene. Critics argue that the lack of independent witnesses, physical evidence, or a verifiable mechanism leaves the case unresolved rather than confirmed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? ## Federico Ibáñez's reported experience

According to later investigations and published accounts, Federico Ibáñez was a 54-year-old farmer from Turís who said he encountered the object while travelling by car towards farmland in the area. The reported event occurred in daylight, which made the case stand out from many UFO reports involving distant lights seen at night.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valenciabonita.es/2015/11/19/ovnis-en-la-historia-reciente-de-valencia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valenciabonita.es">[Valencia Bonita]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valenciabonita.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ovnis en la historia reciente de valencia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Valencia BonitaOVNIS en la historia reciente de Valencia19 Nov 2015 — Federico Ibáñez Ibáñez, un agricultor de 54 años de Turís, marchaba...</span></span></span>

Ibáñez’s account described a large, unusual object positioned near a rural track. He reportedly first interpreted it as something more ordinary before concluding that it did not resemble a normal vehicle. The object was described as having an oval or egg-like shape, and the account included the presence of two small figures that appeared to move towards it before it left the area.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? The detail that made Turís especially influential among UFO researchers was the claimed physical proximity. This was not presented as a distant observation of an unexplained light but as a close-range encounter in which the witness believed he was only a short distance from the object and its occupants. That type of report attracted attention because it allowed investigators to examine the location, distances, and witness descriptions rather than relying only on a brief sighting summary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? The timing also contributed to the case’s later reputation. The encounter occurred only months before the better-known [Manises incident]({{ 'manises-case-f32e0b/' | relative_url }}) in Valencia in November 1979, creating a period in which the province received significant attention from UFO investigators and the media. However, the two cases differ greatly: Manises involved pilots, air traffic personnel and military records, while Turís remained primarily a single-witness close encounter.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manises_UFO_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Manises UFO incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Manises UFO incident</span></span></span>

## Witness testimony and memory

The strongest element of the Turís case is the amount of attention given to the witness interview. Researchers from the Valencian UFO group AVIU reportedly visited the site shortly after the event, questioned Ibáñez in detail and attempted to measure aspects of his description, including the location and dimensions associated with the reported landing site. A later reassessment also revisited the original testimony and investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? Supporters of the case argue that Ibáñez’s behaviour during the investigation was significant. Published accounts by investigators describe him as a person who did not appear to be seeking publicity and who provided a consistent description when questioned at different times. They also highlight that he reportedly identified the location where he believed the object had been present, allowing investigators to examine the physical setting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39622667/IMAGINACION_O_REALIDAD_EL_ATERRIZAJE_DE_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">imaginacion o realidad. el aterrizaje de turisUn amplio informe de investigación se publicó en nuestro libro Los OVNIS y la Cienc...</span></span></span>

However, consistency alone does not prove that an extraordinary event occurred. A witness can honestly describe an experience while still being mistaken about what caused it. Memory research has shown that recollections can remain confident while changing through perception, interpretation and later reinforcement. In UFO investigations, this creates a recurring challenge: the reliability of a witness account and the accuracy of the explanation attached to it are separate questions.

Turís is particularly vulnerable to this issue because there were no independent observers who could confirm the central claims. The investigation documented what Ibáñez said he experienced, but it did not produce external evidence showing that an unknown craft or beings were present. This distinction is why the case remains important as a study of testimony, while its extraordinary interpretation remains disputed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? ## Sceptical and supportive interpretations

For supporters of the UFO interpretation, Turís remains valuable because it combines several features they consider unusual: a daylight encounter, a close-range observation, a detailed description, and an investigation carried out soon after the reported event. They argue that the witness did not simply provide a vague story years later but was interviewed and examined while the memory was still relatively fresh.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? Some researchers have treated Turís as a classic example of a close encounter case, particularly because of the reported interaction between the witness and the alleged occupants. The later study by Vicente-Juan Ballester Olmos presented the case as an opportunity to examine how such reports are formed, preserved and reassessed decades after the original claim.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.academia.edu/39927304/IMAGINATION_OR_REALITY_THE_LANDING_AT_TURIS" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: academia.edu">[Academia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">academia.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">IMAGINATION OR REALITY THE LANDING AT TURIS</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Academia(PDF) IMAGINATION OR REALITY? Sceptical interpretations focus on the limits of the evidence. The investigation relied heavily on Ibáñez’s own description, and no confirmed material trace, photograph, recording or independent witness account has emerged to verify the event.         .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
