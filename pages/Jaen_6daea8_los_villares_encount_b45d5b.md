## Introduction

The Los Villares close encounter is the most famous humanoid-style UFO case associated with Jaén. It centres on the testimony of Dionisio Ávila, a retired farmer from Los Villares who said that on 16 July 1996 he encountered a landed object, three unusual beings and a small inscribed stone that became known as the “lucerillo”. The case attracted national attention because it appeared to offer more than a sighting: a witness account was combined with claims of physical traces, unusual rocks and later investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.orm.es/programas/elultimopeldano/el-ultimo-peldano-el-caso-34-los-villares-34-piedras-y-simbolos-et-los-vampiros-34-masticadores-34-avistamiento-ovni-en-santiago-de-la-ribera/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: orm.es">[orm.es]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">orm.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">EL ÚLTIMO PELDAÑO. Its importance in Jaén’s UFO history comes less from proven evidence than from the unusual way the story developed. Unlike many brief reports of lights in the sky, Los Villares became a long-running investigation involving journalists, UFO researchers, laboratory examinations and competing interpretations. Supporters have treated it as one of Spain’s strongest close-encounter stories, while critics have pointed to the lack of independent confirmation, uncertain provenance of the objects and the possibility of ordinary human explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://caravaca.blogspot.com/2014/03/los-villares-un-encuentro-ovni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: caravaca.blogspot.com">[caravaca.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">caravaca.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">los villares un encuentro ovni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Esos Misteriosos Objetos Celestes y sus Tripulantes: LOS VILLARES: UN ENCUENTRO OVNI SORPRENDENTEMarch 30, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2014</span></span></span>

## Dionisio Ávila’s 1996 account

According to Ávila’s account, the incident occurred outside Los Villares, Jaén, while he was walking with his dog. He described seeing a strange metallic object on the ground, accompanied by three humanoid figures. The object was later described in retellings as a dome-like craft several metres across, with windows or markings, while the beings were said to have worn tight-fitting metallic clothing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elojocritico.info/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/EOC-88-pdf.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elojocritico.info">[elojocritico.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elojocritico.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EOC 88 pdf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Desde 1992...</span></span></span>

The most unusual part of the story was the alleged interaction. Ávila said that he received a small stone bearing symbols from the beings or from the object itself. The symbols, often represented in later accounts as repeating shapes similar to “IOIOI”, became the central mystery around which later investigations developed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Ocultismo/Ocu%20Grupos%20Ocultos/Ocu%20Jimenez%2C%20Iker%20-%20Enigmas%20Sin%20Resolver.PDF" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: maestrosdelsaber.com">[Maestros del Saber]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">maestrosdelsaber.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Maestros del Saber Págs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">PROLOGO A ESTA EDICION 11...</span></span></span>

The account was initially publicised by UFO investigators and journalists, including reports connected with the magazine *Enigmas*. Later, writer and UFO investigator Juan José Benítez investigated the case and helped bring it to a much wider audience through books and television work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://caravaca.blogspot.com/2014/03/los-villares-un-encuentro-ovni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: caravaca.blogspot.com">[caravaca.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">caravaca.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">los villares un encuentro ovni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Esos Misteriosos Objetos Celestes y sus Tripulantes: LOS VILLARES: UN ENCUENTRO OVNI SORPRENDENTEMarch 30, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2014</span></span></span>

From an evidence perspective, the main strength of the case is the consistency of Ávila’s personal testimony over time. The main weakness is that the most extraordinary elements — the craft, beings and communication — depend almost entirely on his account. There were no confirmed independent witnesses to the central encounter and no publicly available physical evidence that demonstrates an extraordinary origin for the objects involved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://elojocritico.info/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/EOC-88-pdf.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: elojocritico.info">[elojocritico.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">elojocritico.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">EOC 88 pdf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Desde 1992...</span></span></span>

## The alleged object and inscribed stone

The “lucerillo” became the defining feature of Los Villares because it appeared to provide something that could be examined rather than simply described. According to the case narrative, Ávila recovered a small stone with strange markings after the encounter. Later investigations focused heavily on its material composition and the way the symbols had been made.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://planetabenitez.com/el-anillo-de-plata-2/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: planetabenitez.com">[Planeta Benitez]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">planetabenitez.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Planeta Benitez El anillo de plata</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Planeta BenitezEl anillo de plata - J.J. Benítez...</span></span></span>

Reports connected with the investigation state that the stone was examined by laboratories and that it was identified as a natural mineral object rather than an unknown material. A later summary of the examinations describes it as a barite nodule, a mineral found in the local area, while analysis of the markings indicated that they had been produced mechanically or manually rather than representing an unknown writing system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archivos.wikanda.es/wikanda/Andaluc%C3%ADa_de_leyenda.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archivos.wikanda.es">[Wikanda Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archivos.wikanda.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Archives Andalucía de leyenda</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikanda ArchivesAndalucía de leyenda...</span></span></span>

For some researchers, the existence of markings remained the most intriguing part of the case because the symbols appeared deliberate. However, deliberate markings do not by themselves establish an extraordinary source. A carved stone can show human activity without proving who made it, when it was made or why. This distinction became one of the main points of disagreement between believers and sceptical investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archivos.wikanda.es/wikanda/Andaluc%C3%ADa_de_leyenda.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archivos.wikanda.es">[Wikanda Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archivos.wikanda.es</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Archives Andalucía de leyenda</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikanda ArchivesAndalucía de leyenda...</span></span></span>

The wider collection of inscribed stones that appeared around the case created additional controversy. Some investigators argued that later stones did not match the original object and may have been produced by people influenced by the publicity surrounding the event. Other researchers treated the stones as part of the continuing mystery of Los Villares.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufologiacba.blogspot.com/2011/01/los-villares-un-encuentro-ovni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologiacba.blogspot.com">[Ufología CBA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologiacba.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufología CBAUFO HUNTERS: Los Villares: Un encuentro OVNI sorprendente</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">January 24, 2011...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 24, 2011</span></span></span>

## Investigations and unanswered questions

The investigation of Los Villares followed a pattern common in famous close-encounter cases: the original witness report was followed by attempts to locate physical confirmation. Researchers examined the location, documented marks in the landscape and studied the stones associated with the story. This moved the case beyond a simple anecdote, but it did not resolve the central question of what Ávila actually experienced.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://caravaca.blogspot.com/2014/03/los-villares-un-encuentro-ovni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: caravaca.blogspot.com">[caravaca.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">caravaca.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">los villares un encuentro ovni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Esos Misteriosos Objetos Celestes y sus Tripulantes: LOS VILLARES: UN ENCUENTRO OVNI SORPRENDENTEMarch 30, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 30, 2014</span></span></span>

One complication is that much of the investigative record comes from UFO researchers or media productions rather than independent scientific studies designed to test extraordinary claims.
