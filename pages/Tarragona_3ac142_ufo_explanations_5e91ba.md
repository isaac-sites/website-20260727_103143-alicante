## Introduction

[Tarragona]({{ 'tarragonas-ufo-cases-between-mystery-and/' | relative_url }})’s best-known UFO reports show why a sighting can be genuine as an experience while still having a conventional explanation. The province’s most important example is the 13 May 1969 incident at Reus Air Base, where controllers, technicians and soldiers reported a bright object near a departing Boeing 727. The case became famous because it involved aviation personnel and an official military investigation, but the surviving file does not establish an unknown craft. Instead, the investigation considered optical and atmospheric explanations, making Reus a useful example of how reflections, distance, lighting and human perception can transform ordinary aerial events into puzzling reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?control=BMDB20160068825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

Looking at explanations does not mean dismissing witnesses. A person can accurately describe a light, shape or movement while still misjudging what produced it. ## Optical effects in the sky

### The Reus 1969 case and the problem of apparent objects

The Reus Air Base sighting remains the clearest example of optical misidentification in Tarragona’s UFO history. The official record describes a bright object seen after a Boeing 727 departed, with several observers reporting a luminous point that appeared stationary. When viewed through binoculars, the object was described as circular or possibly spherical, with brighter areas within the light. The involvement of trained observers made the report significant, but it did not remove the possibility of a visual illusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diaridetarragona.com/reus/1526/el-ejercito-ha-investigado-cuatro-fenomenos-ovni-en-la-provincia-20160213-0017-emdt201602130017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diaridetarragona.com">[Diari de Tarragona]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diaridetarragona.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on diaridetarragona.com.</span></span></span>

The Spanish Air Force investigation examined possible explanations rather than treating the observation itself as proof of an extraordinary object. The released file from the Biblioteca Virtual de Defensa shows that the event was preserved as an official investigation into an unusual aerial observation, while later summaries of the case describe the possibility of an optical effect involving atmospheric conditions and the interaction of sunlight with an aircraft viewed from a particular angle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?control=BMDB20160068825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

This type of explanation is important because bright aerial objects are often judged by appearance rather than distance and geometry. A distant aircraft reflecting sunlight can lose its normal features, especially when seen against a clear sky. At long range, an observer may not perceive wings, fuselage or movement accurately; instead, the eye may register a bright point or unusual shape.

### Reflections, glare and atmospheric distortion

Several mechanisms can create UFO-like appearances without any unusual object being present:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Sunlight reflection:** Aircraft surfaces can briefly reflect the Sun and appear as bright stationary points, especially when the aircraft is travelling towards or away from the observer.
* **Atmospheric refraction:** Layers of air with different temperatures can bend light and slightly alter the apparent position or shape of distant objects.
* **Distance effects:** A faraway aircraft, balloon or satellite may appear motionless because its movement is difficult to judge without reference points.
* **Glare and contrast:** A bright object against a dark or empty sky can appear larger, sharper or more unusual than it really is.

</div>

These factors are particularly relevant to reports involving airports and military areas because the sky contains many legitimate sources of unusual lights: aircraft at different altitudes, navigation lights, reflections and test activity. The [Reus case]({{ 'reus-case/' | relative_url }}) illustrates that a location associated with aviation can increase both the chance of seeing unusual objects and the chance of misinterpreting them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.diaridetarragona.com/reus/1526/el-ejercito-ha-investigado-cuatro-fenomenos-ovni-en-la-provincia-20160213-0017-emdt201602130017.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: diaridetarragona.com">[Diari de Tarragona]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">diaridetarragona.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on diaridetarragona.com.</span></span></span>

NASA guidance on identifying UFO or UAP reports highlights similar problems, noting that bright planets, aircraft, satellites, balloons and camera effects commonly account for many initially unexplained observations. It also emphasises the importance of checking time, location, direction and observing conditions before reaching conclusions.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/night-sky-network/identifying-ufos-and-uaps/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Identifying UFOs and UAPs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASA ScienceIdentifying UFOs and UAPs - NASA ScienceDecember 1, 2013...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1, 2013</span></span></span>

## Limits of human observation

### Why trained witnesses can still misidentify objects

One of the most common misunderstandings in UFO debates is that experienced witnesses cannot make mistakes. Tarragona’s Reus case shows why this assumption is too simple. Air traffic controllers and military personnel may be highly skilled at their jobs, but identifying an unfamiliar object in open sky requires different information: distance, angle, lighting, speed and background references.

Human vision is excellent at detecting changes and bright points, but it is less reliable at estimating size, distance and speed when an object has no clear reference point. A small object nearby and a large object far away can appear similar. A moving aircraft viewed head-on may seem stationary because the angle changes slowly, while a distant object moving across the sky may appear to accelerate because of the observer’s own movement or changing visual focus.

The same issue appears in official UFO archives internationally. Many government investigations recorded genuine reports from credible observers, but later analysis often identified familiar causes such as planets, aircraft, weather balloons or satellites. The existence of a report demonstrates that an observation occurred; it does not automatically demonstrate that the original interpretation was correct.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives UFO reports</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesUFO reports - The National Archives...</span></span></span>

### The role of memory and later interpretation

UFO reports are often reconstructed after the event through conversations, newspaper coverage or repeated retelling. Over time, descriptions may become more precise or more dramatic, even when the original observation was brief. This does not mean witnesses are deliberately exaggerating; memory naturally changes as people try to explain unusual experiences.

For Tarragona cases, this matters because the strongest evidence usually comes from contemporary records: official reports, witness statements recorded at the time, weather information and aviation data. Later interpretations can add useful context, but they may also introduce assumptions that were not part of the original sighting.

## Unresolved versus explained cases

A useful distinction in Tarragona’s UFO history is between **explained**, **unresolved**, and **unsupported** reports.

An **explained case** is one where investigators can match the observation to a likely cause, such as an aircraft reflection, astronomical object or atmospheric effect. The Reus 1969 case is often discussed in this category because the official investigation favoured an optical explanation rather than evidence of an unknown vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?control=BMDB20160068825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

An **unresolved case** does not necessarily mean an extraordinary explanation. It usually means the available information is insufficient: perhaps there is no photograph, no radar record, incomplete timing information or too few details to test competing explanations.

An **unsupported claim** is different again. It refers to reports where the available evidence does not allow the main claims to be verified, even if the story remains popular in local memory.

This approach helps explain why Tarragona’s UFO record contains both intriguing stories and ordinary explanations. The province’s aviation links, coastal skies and changing technology have produced many opportunities for unusual observations, but careful investigation often shows that the mystery lies in perception rather than in the object itself.

## What Tarragona’s cases reveal about UFO investigations

The main lesson from Tarragona is not that every UFO report has an obvious answer. Some observations remain uncertain because information is lost or incomplete. The more important point is that investigation changes the quality of a case. A bright object seen near Reus Air Base initially raised questions because of its witnesses and setting, but examination of lighting, aircraft position and atmospheric conditions shifted the discussion towards a plausible optical explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es/BVMDefensa/exp_ovni/es/consulta/registro.do?control=BMDB20160068825" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es">[Biblioteca Virtual Defensa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bibliotecavirtual.defensa.gob.es</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on gob.es.</span></span></span>

Optical misidentifications are therefore not a footnote in Tarragona’s UFO history; they are central to understanding it. They show how ordinary physical effects can create extraordinary impressions, why witness testimony needs technical context, and why the strongest UFO investigations focus on evidence that can distinguish an unknown observation from a known phenomenon.

    return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
